FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum order?

10 Pods per order. Larger orders move you into lower price tiers automatically.

How do price tiers work for reorders?

We add up the Pods you've ordered over the last 6 months. If that total reaches the next tier, your next order is priced at that tier.

Is shipping really free?

Yes. Shipping is included on every wholesale order to US practices.

Can we include the Pod in treatment instead of selling it?

Yes. Many practices include a Pod with aligner or retainer treatment. Wholesale pricing is the same either way.

Do we have to charge $99.95?

You set your in-office price. If you advertise the Pod online, we ask partners not to list it below the $99.95 suggested retail price.

How do we pay?

Approved practices order and pay by card through their wholesale account. Multi-location groups can ask us about invoicing.
