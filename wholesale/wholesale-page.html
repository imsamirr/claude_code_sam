Good to know

Wholesale questions, answered.

Who can apply?

Licensed dental practices in the US: orthodontists, general and cosmetic dentists, prosthodontists, denture clinics, dental sleep practices, and multi-location groups. We may ask for your practice license or NPI number to verify.

What's the minimum order?

Opening orders start at 12 units. Reorders start at 6 units.

What are the payment terms?

Payment is due on your first order. Net-30 terms are available for established accounts.

Can I set my own retail price?

Yes. We share a suggested retail price of $99.95 and ask partners not to advertise below it online. In your practice, you can sell it, bundle it into treatment fees, or include it as a gift.

What if a patient's Pod has a problem?

Every Pod carries our 120-day money-back guarantee. Patients can contact us directly, or you can send them our way and we handle it.

Will there be another stockist near me?

We look at every area case by case so partner practices aren't competing with each other on the same street.

Can I use the Pod in-office instead of reselling it?

Yes. Some practices use Pods chairside to clean appliances during appointments. Mention it in your application and we'll quote for in-office use.

What happens after I apply?

A member of our wholesale team reviews every application personally and gets back to you within 2 business days with pricing and next steps.
