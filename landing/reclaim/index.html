Frequently Asked Questions

Who is RECLAIM for?

Adult women with full lives and busy minds, especially in their late 30s to 50s, who want a simple daily stress-support ritual. Check the label for intended use, directions and cautions.

When is the best time to drink RECLAIM?

Whenever your day gets loudest. Many women pick the afternoon slump or the hour after the kids are in bed. Taking it at the same time every day makes the habit easier to keep. Follow the label’s directions.

Will it make me drowsy?

RECLAIM is being developed for daily use, not as a sleep aid. Individual responses depend on the final formula and your circumstances. Follow the label’s cautions.

Is RECLAIM a sleep product?

No. It’s a daily calm and stress-support drink. It isn’t a sleep medication or a treatment for insomnia.

Will RECLAIM help with bloating?

Use only if substantiated and authorized.

RECLAIM helps relieve bloating as described on its label. Results vary, and bloating can have different causes. Persistent or unexplained symptoms deserve individual medical advice.

Does feeling stressed mean I have high cortisol?

Not necessarily. Feeling stressed, bloated or tired doesn’t establish that you have high cortisol. RECLAIM isn’t a diagnostic test or a treatment for a cortisol disorder.

Does RECLAIM balance my hormones?

No. RECLAIM doesn’t balance estrogen or progesterone or treat a hormonal condition. Its focus is daily stress support.

Can I use it during perimenopause or with HRT?

Check with your healthcare professional that the final ingredients are right for you, especially if you use HRT or other medication. RECLAIM doesn’t replace prescribed care.
