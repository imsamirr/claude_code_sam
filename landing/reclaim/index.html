Frequently Asked Questions

Who is RECLAIM for?

RECLAIM is designed with adult women’s busy routines in mind, particularly those looking for a simple daily stress-support ritual. Check the final label for intended users, directions, and cautions.

Is RECLAIM a sleep product?

No. Its intended role is daily calm and stress support. It isn’t a sleep medication or a treatment for insomnia.

Will RECLAIM help with bloating?

Use this answer only if substantiated and authorized.

RECLAIM helps relieve bloating as described on its label. Results vary, and bloating can have different causes. Persistent or unexplained symptoms deserve individual medical advice.

If a bloat claim is not supported, remove the benefit from the page and packaging rather than implying that stress support guarantees digestive relief.

Does RECLAIM balance my hormones?

RECLAIM should not be described as balancing estrogen, progesterone, or treating a hormonal condition unless specifically supported and authorized. Its intended focus is daily stress support.

Can I use it during perimenopause?

Check with your healthcare professional to confirm that the final ingredients are appropriate for you, especially if you use HRT or other medication. RECLAIM does not replace prescribed care.

Does feeling stressed mean I have high cortisol?

No. Feeling stressed, bloated, or tired does not establish that you have high cortisol. RECLAIM is not a diagnostic test or a treatment for a cortisol disorder.

Will it make me drowsy?

The product is being developed for daily use rather than as a sleep aid. Individual responses depend on the final formula and your circumstances. Follow the label’s cautions.
