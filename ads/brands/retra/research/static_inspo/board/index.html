Format ideas for Retra

The recurring formats across all five brands, grouped by funnel stage. Red notes mark formats that only work if the claim behind them is true for Retra.

TOF Top of funnel 16 formats

  • Native screenshot

    An iMessage group chat, a text thread, an X/Threads post or an Instagram comment over a photo. The product appears small, at the bottom.

    Seen at KLIK, Camp Snap, Retropia

    Use real customer words or make it obviously the brand talking; invented 'customer' chats count as fake testimonials.

  • Headline over a sample photo

    One emotional or provocative line in an editorial serif above a full candid photo, with a small product badge.

    Seen at Retropia

    Needs real Retra sample photos to be honest.

  • Minimal hero + handwritten line

    One camera on a flat background, two or three handwritten lines, the wordmark at the bottom.

    Seen at KLIK

    Easiest to produce from our existing cut-outs.

  • Hands + tower or stack

    Hands holding a stack of colourways, big type such as '∞ shots', an icon checklist beside it.

    Seen at KLIK

  • Stat swap or receipt

    A giant number swap (27 shots per roll → 500 per charge) or a till receipt where everything costs $0 except the camera.

    Seen at KLIK

    Only use numbers we can back up.

  • Pattern-interrupt prop

    The camera frozen in ice, in a produce bag ('Oops, I bought it again'), inside a retro OS pop-up window, on a CRT TV screen.

    Seen at Flashback, Camp Snap

    Highest scroll-stop; cheap to render.

  • Occasion gift moment

    The camera with a grad cap, a bow, holiday props or kids' hands, and the occasion named in the headline.

    Seen at KLIK, Camp Snap

  • Activity split

    One half is the activity (climbing, a festival crowd, summer camp), the other half the camera with three benefit icons.

    Seen at Flashback, Camp Snap

  • Colour drop as news

    One colourway on a matching gradient treated like a launch ('Gone twice. Back again.'), or a colour-personality chart.

    Seen at Camp Snap, KLIK

  • Era nostalgia

    A still life with period props, a blueprint line drawing, or a 'Born in the '70s, reborn digital' heritage hook.

    Seen at Camp Snap

    Avoid branded props (no Game Boy logos or the like).

  • Real UGC + caption bar

    A real customer photo (tattoos, mirror selfies) with a one-line editorial caption.

    Seen at Flashback, Rewindpix

    Needs real customers.

  • Personal callout or question

    A name or a direct question as the headline ('Are you over-editing?', 'You haven't taken a real photo in years').

    Seen at Retropia, KLIK

  • Build-up series

    A run of statics that each add one thing ('First you got the camera… then the strap… and finally the filters').

    Seen at Retropia

    Works as a sequence across one ad set.

  • Venn diagram

    Phone in one circle, disposable in the other, the camera in the overlap.

    Seen at Flashback

  • Viewfinder POV

    A shot through the viewfinder onto a scene: you see exactly what you shoot.

    Seen at Rewindpix

  • Magazine cover

    A big masthead word over a hand-held product in a real place.

    Seen at Rewindpix

MOF Middle of funnel 8 formats

  • Comparison table

    Disposable vs reusable in two columns: cost, shots, reuse.

    Seen at KLIK

  • Filter bands on one photo

    One portrait cut into horizontal bands, each band a different filter, labelled.

    Seen at Camp Snap

  • One photo × every filter

    The same photo repeated in each filter as a grid.

    Seen at Flashback

  • Everyone else vs us

    Two Instagram-style posts side by side; ours gets the heart.

    Seen at Retropia

  • Icon feature grid

    The product plus four icon features, or a four-panel close-up grid.

    Seen at Camp Snap, Flashback

  • 'You asked. We listened.'

    UI slider rows pairing each customer request with what changed.

    Seen at Camp Snap

    Only if the requests are real.

  • Filter spec card

    A big filter name, three sample photos and tags, like a film-stock card.

    Seen at Rewindpix

  • Risk reversal

    A giant '30-day' return guarantee next to the product.

    Seen at KLIK

    Matches Retra's real policy before running.

BOF Bottom of funnel 7 formats

  • Sold-out lineup

    Every colour in a row, real SOLD OUT stamps on some, 'In stock' tags on the rest, price below.

    Seen at KLIK, Camp Snap

    Stamp only colours that are actually out.

  • Supermarket tray

    The camera shrink-wrapped on a meat tray with a barcode, '+1 free gift' sticker and 'sells out fast'.

    Seen at Flashback, KLIK

  • Founder or plain-text letter

    An email-style note from the founder or team with the offer inside.

    Seen at KLIK, Retropia

  • Tiered bundle cards

    Buy 1 / Buy 2 / Buy 3 cards with the gift at each tier.

    Seen at Flashback

    Natural fit for Retra's Buy 2, get 1 free.

  • Bundle flat lay + gift value

    Camera, case, strap and cap laid out, with 'Free gift valued at $X, for a limited time only'.

    Seen at Flashback

  • Circled price

    A struck-through old price with the new price circled by hand, plus a best-seller badge.

    Seen at Retropia, KLIK

    Compare-at price must be real.

  • Pricing-glitch letter

    'We're sorry for the mix-up': honouring a mistaken low price while stock lasts.

    Seen at Retropia

    Only if it really happened; otherwise it's a fake-scarcity claim.
