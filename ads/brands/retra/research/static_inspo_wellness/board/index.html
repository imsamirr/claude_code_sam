What stands out

  1. Plain text wins long. Letters, apologies and story cards with no design run for months: Bioma's "if you start this month" card has run 400 days, Hears' cartoon sale ad 199, Loop's iOS pop-up 183.
  2. Native beats polished for TOF. Every brand except Dore & Rose leans on screenshots, caption stickers, Post-its and handwriting. These are cheap to make from our existing cut-outs.
  3. The two groups split. Sleep and hearing brands sell with product-photo craft, personas and colour drops. The health brands sell with cartoons, timelines, enemies and apology sales.
  4. Borrow formats, not claims. Most health ads rest on weight-loss or medical claims and body before/afters. Copy the layout and write Retra's own true line (through StefanBrain).

Format ideas

Recurring formats across all ten brands, by funnel stage. Plain notes say how a format could fit Retra; red notes are rules to follow before using it.

TOF Top of funnel 18 formats

  • Plain-text letter or statement

    An "official apology", "we got copied" or a customer's story set as a letter on plain paper. No product photo, or a small one at the end.

    Seen at Hears, Grüns, Bioma, Primal Queen

    For Retra: "An official apology to disposable cameras" style. Copy from StefanBrain.

  • Native screenshot

    iMessage between friends or family, an X post, an Instagram or TikTok comment reply, even a ChatGPT answer recommending the product.

    Seen at Dore & Rose, Arrae, Manta, Grüns

    Use real customer words or make the brand the obvious speaker. Invented customer chats are fake testimonials.

  • UGC photo + caption overlay

    A phone photo with a "POV:" bar, TikTok caption stickers or a handwritten-style line on top.

    Seen at Hears, Ozlo, Arrae, Grüns, Create

    Needs real customer or creator photos.

  • Analogue notes

    Post-its on a bedside table, a handwritten note by the product, a whiteboard drawing, handwritten arrows drawn over a photo.

    Seen at Hears, Loop, Primal Queen, Ozlo, Arrae

    Cheap to make. Good fit for Retra's film-era feel.

  • Cartoon or comic

    A comic strip of one customer's story, a memoji 2x2 of the alternatives, a two-panel meme, 3D cartoon characters, a pencil sketch.

    Seen at Bioma, Hears, Manta, Primal Queen, Grüns

    Draw our own characters. Don't copy film or toy IP (Gru, Shrek, Minions, Barbie appear in the originals).

  • Surreal prop or scene

    Product in a vending machine, a bed in the middle of a crosswalk, a billboard in the clouds, product floating in a pool, a thermal-camera image.

    Seen at Hears, Ozlo, Create, Bioma, Primal Queen

    Highest scroll-stop. Easy to render with our cut-outs.

  • Reframe or enemy line

    One sentence that renames the problem or picks a villain: "Not polyester pretending to be soft." "You're not lazy." "[Alternative]? Nah."

    Seen at Dore & Rose, Grüns, Primal Queen, Bioma, Hears, Manta

    For Retra the villain is the phone camera or the disposable. Lines from StefanBrain.

  • Objection as the headline

    The reader's own pushback in quotes, answered by the picture: "I don't do earplugs." with a split of the Saturday with and without.

    Seen at Hears

  • Persona or occasion callout

    Name who it's for or when: night shift, college dorm, festival weekend, the snoring boyfriend, a gift for the light sleeper.

    Seen at Manta, Ozlo, Hears

    Name the persona every ad is written for.

  • Press or authority quote card

    A press quote in a frosted card, "Better than [big brand]", "The Rolex of…", a gift-list badge, an "As seen in" bar.

    Seen at Manta, Ozlo, Dore & Rose

    Only real press and real customer quotes.

  • Data or app UI as the visual

    A sound-meter card, a sleep-score before and after, a line chart, a big stat headline.

    Seen at Hears, Ozlo, Manta, Primal Queen, Grüns

    For Retra: photo counts per charge or per trip, never invented stats.

  • Timeline

    Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 21, month-by-month, "If you start this month, by Christmas you'll…", a week-by-week caption.

    Seen at Bioma (400 days live), Arrae, Primal Queen

    For Retra: a trip or a year in photos.

  • Split or visual metaphor

    Before/after, "your Saturday on nothing vs on ours", two shifts with two different results, a dried fruit vs a juicy one.

    Seen at Hears, Manta, Primal Queen, Bioma

    Body before/after shots are a Meta policy risk; object metaphors are safe.

  • Listicle card

    "5 things that happen when…", "Things I found out in my 40s", stacked "Want…? Want…?" questions, "One product, five problems solved."

    Seen at Ozlo, Grüns, Arrae, Bioma

  • Photo-only, no copy

    A plain packshot, a lifestyle crop, a lo-fi top-down phone photo, an archival black-and-white photo.

    Seen at Dore & Rose, Ozlo, Primal Queen

    Some of the longest runners (Ozlo 138 days, Primal Queen 90).

  • Colour drop or collab as news

    A new colour on a matching texture, "Just dropped", "Limited edition", a co-designed edition with a partner.

    Seen at Loop, Dore & Rose, Hears

    Fits Retra's colourways.

  • Pun or wordplay

    "Grape Expectations", "Pleasing to the eye. And the ears.", circular type round the product ("Best. Earplugs. Ever.").

    Seen at Create, Loop, Manta

  • Warning or reverse psychology

    "DO NOT take this if…⚠️", "Don't buy these if…", a parody of the sensitive-content blur screen.

    Seen at Primal Queen

MOF Middle of funnel 8 formats

  • Ours vs theirs cards

    Two cards or columns: the cheap/old way with ✗ marks, ours with ✓ marks. Or a table over a lifestyle photo.

    Seen at Manta, Primal Queen, Create, Hears

    Retra vs disposable vs phone.

  • Chooser or quiz

    "Which one do you need?" product cards, "Take our quiz" grid, "Is it right for you?" icon row.

    Seen at Hears, Loop, Arrae

    For Retra: which colour or which filter is you.

  • Spec labels round the product

    Feature pills floating around the product, a basket of models with specs, stacked product with big-number callouts.

    Seen at Loop, Ozlo, Grüns

    500 photos per charge, no Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/app.

  • Problems / solution columns

    A list of problems on the left, the product on the right.

    Seen at Arrae

  • Venn diagram

    Three benefits as circles, the product in the overlap.

    Seen at Grüns

  • Objection first

    Lead with the doubt ("Yes, it's pricey") then show what's inside and the price.

    Seen at Bioma

  • Crossed-out list

    Old problems or alternatives struck through, then "Meet…" or "Purpose-built for…".

    Seen at Ozlo, Primal Queen

  • Launch numeral or range overview

    A giant "2" behind the product, the whole collection as flat illustrations, every flavour in a grid.

    Seen at Ozlo, Dore & Rose, Create

BOF Bottom of funnel 8 formats

  • Apology or sell-out letter

    "We're so sorry" (selling fast, sale ending, forgot to tell you), "We're shutting it down", "Clearance: we're no longer making these colours".

    Seen at Grüns, Bioma, Primal Queen, Ozlo, Arrae, Dore & Rose

    Only true scarcity. Retra really is low on stock.

  • Cartoon offer

    A sketch of the customer "waiting for a sale" ("This is the sale."), a "SOLD OUT" monster grabbing someone.

    Seen at Hears (199 days live), Grüns

  • Pay for 2, get 3

    A colour lineup lit on black, stacked offer banners, two hands holding three cases.

    Seen at Hears, Loop

    Natural fit for Retra's Buy 2, get 1 free.

  • Price card + gift list

    A struck-through price, a per-day price sticker, and free gifts / free shipping / guarantee as a ticked list.

    Seen at Dore & Rose, Grüns, Bioma, Primal Queen

    Compare-at prices must be real. No currency code on images.

  • Packaging as the offer

    The bundle shown as a retail blister pack with a price starburst.

    Seen at Loop, Manta

  • Native pop-up offer

    An iOS permission dialog: "would like to share 20% off + a free gift", Decline / Accept.

    Seen at Loop (183 days live)

  • Lo-fi scrawl

    An MS-Paint handwritten sale ad: "…sorry, our designer is on vacation".

    Seen at Primal Queen (82 days live)

  • Gift with purchase / occasion sale

    Product + free item joined by a "+", a birthday cake built from the product, "It's our birthday and we lowered the price", HSA/FSA reminders before year-end.

    Seen at Ozlo, Primal Queen, Grüns

    HSA/FSA is a Comfort Wear angle, not Retra.

Selection: for each brand, image ads ordered by Atria impression rank, one row per creative, 40 candidates, then 20 picked for format variety at 14 top / 3 middle / 3 bottom of funnel. For the eight brands beyond Hears and Dore & Rose, only ads live 14+ days were considered; Loop was limited to English ads. Days running is how long Atria has seen each ad live. Ostrichpillow was checked but isn't in Atria; Ozlo Sleep took its place. Several originals use licensed or borrowed IP (Shrek, Minions, Gru, Barbie-style dolls) and many make weight-loss or medical claims: the format notes say what to keep and what to leave.
