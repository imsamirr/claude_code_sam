## Strategist's Note

### 1. Winning Foundation & Performance Provenance
This brief directly adapts our number one current prospecting winner, **COM284** ("We asked 500 plus-sized women who have been to Disney one simple question" / $6,130 CAD spend, 2.58 ROAS, 153 purchases, headline "2026's Favorite Shoes For Plus-Sized Traveling", later scaled as COM388 for Black Friday). 

We are transplanting COM284's proven direct-response mechanics from theme park travel to holiday NYC travel (Thanksgiving through Christmas and New Year's), where walking demands are equally unforgiving:
- **Proven Survey Hook Frame:** The opening immediately establishes high-volume third-party consensus ("We asked 500 plus-sized women traveling to NYC one simple question..."). It bypasses typical ad blindness by opening as a crowd-sourced investigation rather than a product pitch.
- **Aggressive Competitor Shoe Teardown (COM284 + COM320):** Instead of polite generalities, the copy directly names the $90 to $180 household running brands (Hoka, On Cloud, Skechers, Nike) and breaks down exactly why their engineering fails a woman carrying extra weight on hard city pavement:
  - *Hoka ($165):* Ultra-thick squishy foam causes foot wobble on uneven curbs and subway steps, shifting strain directly into knees and lower back.
  - *On Cloud ($180):* Rigid, triangle-shaped toe box crushes the metatarsals and squeezes bunions, especially when feet swell by mid-afternoon.
  - *Skechers ($90):* Soft memory foam bottoms out completely after two hours under body weight, leaving you walking on flat rubber with zero arch support.
- **Reddit / Traveler Consensus Bridge:** Validates the discovery through unfiltered peer communities ("hundreds of reviews on Reddit") rather than brand claims.
- **Lost-Moment Emotional Stakes (COM49 + COM365 + COM410):** Connects shoe failure to high-stakes emotional loss. You did not spend thousands on flights, Broadway tickets, and midtown hotel rooms just to sit on a bed with an ice bucket while your family visits Rockefeller Center without you.
- **Mechanism Contrast:** Directly contrasts failed squish with the Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe's structural pillars: firm anatomical arch support that does not quit at 2 p.m., wide stretch-knit toe box that expands as feet swell, rocker midsole for shock absorption on concrete, slip-resistant grip for wet subway tiles, and elastic no-tie laces.
- **Price Anchor & Black Friday Close:** Anchors our $60 sale price against $160 to $180 sneakers and $300 custom orthotics, backed by our ironclad 30-day money-back guarantee.

### 2. Production & Execution Guidelines
- **Format:** Native UGC direct-response video ad. Fast-paced talking head shot on an iPhone (front camera or back camera with natural lighting) intercut with authentic, handheld B-roll.
- **Tone:** Real-person advice, empathetic, direct, slightly fed up with mainstream shoe brands. Zero polished marketing jargon.
- **Visuals & B-Roll:** 
  - Creator talking directly into camera, mid-stride on city streets or in a hotel room preparing to head out.
  - Authentic city walking footage: concrete pavement, subway stairs, bustling sidewalks.
  - Physical shoe demonstrations: flexing the sole to show firm support vs floppy competitors, pressing down on memory foam that stays crushed, pointing out the wide toe box compared to narrow pointed sneakers, slipping feet in effortlessly using the pull tab without bending over.
  - Zero stock footage. Avoid pristine runway aesthetics. Must feel 100% native to Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- **Text On Screen (OST):** High-contrast captions highlighting key hooks, competitor names, prices, and feature callouts. Keep text snappy and readable on mobile screens without cluttering the frame.
- **Table Structure:** Strict two-column table (Script | Visual) with no timestamp column, adhering strictly to Samir's guidelines.

## Hook Variations

### Hook 1 (Concept Lead - Proven Survey Winner)
- **Spoken Audio:** "We asked 500 plus-sized women traveling to NYC one simple question: what shoes do they wear? The answer was almost always the same."
- **Visual:** Creator on a bustling Manhattan sidewalk in a warm winter jacket, speaking directly into the phone camera while pedestrians walk by in the background. Quick cut to a shot looking down at shoes pounding concrete pavement.
- **On-Screen Text (OST):** We asked 500 plus-sized women traveling to NYC one question...
- **Why It Works:** Directly transplants the proven survey premise from COM284 ($6,130 CAD, 2.58 ROAS). It taps into crowd-sourced curiosity and social proof, immediately hooking travelers planning holiday trips to New York.

### Hook 2 (High-Stakes Disqualification - The Vacation Ruiner)
- **Spoken Audio:** "If you are a woman over 200 pounds heading to NYC for the holidays, packing the wrong shoes will ruin your entire trip."
- **Visual:** Creator in a hotel room holding up an oversized, bulky running shoe with a skeptical expression, then cutting to feet slowly limping up subway stairs.
- **On-Screen Text (OST):** Over 200 lbs heading to NYC? Read this before you pack.
- **Why It Works:** Adapts COM284's second highest-performing hook ("If you're a woman over 200lbs..."). Calls out the exact avatar and weight bracket, introducing severe stakes (a ruined vacation) that force the viewer to watch the teardown.

### Hook 3 (Contrarian Peer Advice - Body Reality Callout)
- **Spoken Audio:** "Please stop taking NYC walking shoe advice from 20-year-old influencers who do not walk in your body."
- **Visual:** Creator shaking her head candidly at the camera, tapping her temple, then cutting to a side-by-side comparison of a narrow trendy sneaker versus a roomy comfort shoe.
- **On-Screen Text (OST):** Stop taking shoe advice from people who do not walk in your body.
- **Why It Works:** Modeled on COM284's contrarian hook. Directly calls out the expectation gap where plus-sized women over 50 buy trendy sneakers recommended online only to suffer severe foot, heel, and knee pain.

## Production & Delivery Notes

### Recommended Colorways to Feature
- **Lead Hero Colorway:** Black Purple (our primary winning colorway in visual ads).
- **Secondary Options:** All Black (sleek city look, versatile for travel outfits), Grey Pink (casual walking look), Burgundy (seasonal holiday tone).

### Audio & Voiceover Direction
- **Voice Profile:** Relatable, experienced traveler, female, age 50 to 60. Natural American accent.
- **Delivery:** Confident, warm, direct, speaking woman-to-woman. The delivery should feel like an honest recommendation from an experienced friend who travels often, not an announcer or fitness instructor.
- **Pacing:** Conversational but brisk. Emphasize physical sensations: "pounding", "wobble", "narrow triangle", "compresses completely flat", "zero give".

### Compliance & Claims Guardrails
- **Guarantee:** 30-day money-back guarantee (full refund within 30 days). Do not mention 45 days, store credit, or shipping speed.
- **Approved Medical Claims:** "Podiatrist-recommended" and "Doctor-recommended" are approved. Zero mentions of "orthopedic approved", no fake doctor names, and no invented clinical studies.
- **Competitor Naming:** Named competitor shoes (Hoka, On Cloud, Skechers) are approved in the spoken copy. Do not show clear logos on camera, and use representative silhouettes or blur brand logos.
- **Offer Congruence:** Exclusively state "Black Friday 60% off sitewide through Dec 5". Do not mix with the bundle or free gift promotion.
