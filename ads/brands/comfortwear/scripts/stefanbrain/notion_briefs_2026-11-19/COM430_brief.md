### Production & Filming Guide
- **Aesthetic:** Native UGC shot on an iPhone in 4K at 60fps. Handheld, natural lighting in a real home (kitchen, living room, bedroom). No studio ring lights, no polished ad-agency sheen.
- **B-Roll Requirements:**
  - Split-screen mockups showing mobile survey questions and real-looking response graphs.
  - Close-up B-roll of the creator stepping barefoot onto hardwood floors and wincing.
  - Physical teardown: creator holding up competitor shoes (Hoka, Nike, Skechers) and demonstrating their narrow toe boxes, stiff soles, and squished foam.
  - Shoe demo: showing the Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe flexing, bending, the roomy toe box expanding, removing the anatomical arch support insole, and sliding the shoe on hands-free with the elastic no-tie laces and pull tab.
  - Walking footage: steady, confident strides on pavement and hardwood, wearing everyday casual clothes.
- **Strict Brand Rules:**
  - Only women wear the shoe on camera. Men may only be mentioned as gift-givers or supportive family members.
  - No timestamps in the script table.
  - No em dashes anywhere in on-screen text, captions, or script lines.
  - No claims of curing or treating medical conditions. Use "doctor-recommended" and "podiatrist-recommended."
  - No fake doctor names, no invented star counts or review numbers.
