# Concept COM439: Nurse & Plus-Sized Disney Mirror Try-On

## 1. Strategist's Note

### Reference Ad Overview
- **Reference URL:** [Atria m1439117661664398](https://app.tryatria.com/ad/m1439117661664398)
- **Reference Product:** Viasox Compression Socks (19.5s run time, active 13 days, rank 50 of 986)
- **Core Reference Mechanics:** The reference video features no voiceover and no spoken audio. It relies entirely on a catchy background track, handheld camera POV looking down at legs and feet in front of a floor mirror on carpet, and real-time physical try-on actions (stepping, turning side to side, tugging the cuff, walking in sneakers vs. socks). It feels completely unpolished, native, and real.

### Why This Format Converts
1. **Zero Ad Odor:** It mimics an everyday TikTok or Instagram Reel try-on haul ("look what just arrived in the mail") rather than a staged direct-response shoe commercial.
2. **Instant Sensory Proof:** The viewer sees real feet in the shoe within second one. The elasticity of the upper, the heel bounce, and the roomy toe box are demonstrated physically rather than merely described.
3. **Sound-Off Performance:** Because there is no voiceover, 100% of the message is delivered through high-contrast on-screen text overlays matched to physical product demonstrations. It stops scrollers who browse with sound muted while still feeling energetic with music on.

### Production & Editing Direction
- **Camera Setup & Framing:** Vertical 9:16 ratio. Handheld phone camera held at waist or chest height, angled down toward a full-length floor mirror on bedroom carpet or hardwood floor. The creator captures both the reflection in the mirror and a direct downward angle of her feet.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Warm, natural room light (bedroom, hallway, or near a window). Avoid sterile studio setups or ring light reflections in the mirror. The space should look lived-in and real.
- **Creator Actions & B-Roll Micro-Shots:**
  - *Slip-in test:* Foot slides into the shoe hands-free in one smooth movement, showing the elastic no-tie laces expanding and snapping back.
  - *Heel cushion compression:* Creator steps firmly onto her heel, rocking back to visibly compress the thick cushion sole and rebound.
  - *Wide toe box flex:* Creator points her foot toward the camera and wiggles her toes freely inside the toe box, demonstrating zero side bulge or pinching.
  - *Upper stretch tug:* Two fingers tugging the breathable stretch-knit material upward to show how it stretches over bunions and swollen insteps.
  - *Grip check & strut:* Creator turns side to side in front of the mirror, showing the sleek profile, then takes 2-3 firm steps to demonstrate the slip-resistant grip.
- **Text Overlay Styling:** High-contrast text placed in the mobile safe zone (middle-upper third of the frame). Use large, clean, bold sans-serif lettering (white text on a subtle dark pill or high-contrast drop shadow) designed specifically for 50 to 65+ readers.
- **Audio:** Upbeat, trending lo-fi or chill pop background beat with subtle natural room Foley retained (the satisfying step on the carpet, the stretch of the knit).
