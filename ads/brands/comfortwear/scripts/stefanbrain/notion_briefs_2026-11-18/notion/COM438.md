## 1. Strategist's Note

### Reference Ad
* **Source:** Viasox Ankle Compression Socks (Atria ad `m1670935501286859`, https://app.tryatria.com/ad/m1670935501286859)
* **Performance Context:** 48s video, launched Nov 12, scaling rapidly (ranked #57 of 986 in Atria library).
* **Reference Mechanics:** A continuous, single-take handheld talking-head on the factory floor. No B-roll cutaways. A persistent high-contrast top banner stays locked on screen ("🚨 Black Friday Sale 🚨 / Buy 2 Get 3 Free!"), while the speaker holds up product samples in packaging and explains the ergonomic fix directly to camera. It creates immediate behind-the-scenes authenticity and legitimate operational urgency.

### Production & Creative Direction
* **Visual Style & Setting:** E-commerce fulfillment warehouse floor. Steel racking filled with shoe boxes, pallets, packing tables, and ambient movement in the background (boxes being scanned/packed).
* **AI Avatar Setup:** Female warehouse manager / operations lead, age 35 to 45, dressed in practical warehouse gear (safety vest over a neutral long-sleeve tee or brand polo, hair pulled back, natural makeup). She speaks in a brisk, friendly, no-nonsense tone directly to camera.
* **Camera Movement:** Continuous handheld feel with subtle micro-movements (no rigid AI tripod lock). Keep the avatar in a medium shot (waist up), holding and demonstrating the Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe in hand.
* **Pacing & Edit:** Zero B-roll cuts. The power of this format is uninterrupted direct eye contact. Let the physical shoe in her hands do all the visual demonstration work.
* **Persistent Top Banner:** Pinned to the top 15% of the frame for the entire duration:  
  `🚨 BLACK FRIDAY SALE 🚨`  
  `60% OFF SITEWIDE` (Bold white text on high-visibility red or yellow background).
* **Captions:** Lower-third bold all-caps text with black outline (yellow/orange active word highlight), synced karaoke-style to spoken audio.
