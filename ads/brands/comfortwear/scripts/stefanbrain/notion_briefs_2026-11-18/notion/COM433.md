## 1. Strategist's Note

### Reference Ad Overview
* **Reference Link:** https://app.tryatria.com/ad/m2559942994472915 (Hike Footwear, Atria ID: m2559942994472915)
* **Reference Performance:** Active 33+ days, impression rank #83 of 1,615 in brand account.
* **Core Psychological Mechanism:** Reverse-psychology / altruistic gatekeeping. Telling viewers "please do not buy this" cuts through ad fatigue and completely disarms commercial skepticism. The viewer instantly leans in: "Why is she telling me not to buy?" By framing the scarcity around elderly family members who genuinely need mobility support ("leave them for the seniors who are one slip away from a walker"), the ad creates high moral justification for purchasing multiple pairs immediately while the Black Friday price drop is live.

### How to Produce This Asset

#### 1. Creator Selection & Casting
* **Creator Profile:** Female, 45 to 55 years old. Real adult daughter persona (warm, relatable, protective, authentic family caregiver). She looks and sounds like an everyday mom or daughter, not a fitness influencer or polished TikTok model.
* **Secondary Visual Subject (B-Roll):** Elderly mother (65 to 75+ years old), authentic senior grandma appearance. Real movement: showing early morning mobility hesitation, gripping hallway walls/furniture, followed by effortless walking once wearing the shoes.

#### 2. Filming Environment & Lighting
* **Setting:** Real lived-in suburban home. Kitchen counter or dining table for the selfie A-roll; hallway, living room, and front porch for the B-roll.
* **Lighting:** Natural daylight from windows. Avoid professional three-point studio lighting or harsh ring lights in the eyes, which destroy UGC credibility.

#### 3. Framing & Camera Movement
* **A-Roll (Talking Head):** Handheld smartphone (iPhone style), chest-up framing, eye-level. Slight natural camera float is encouraged.
* **B-Roll (Product & Action):**
  * Handheld close-up unboxing and holding the shoe to camera.
  * Tactile demos: bending the sole to show arch flex, pressing the heel cushion with a thumb, stretching the breathable knit upper, showing the slip-resistant rubber tread pattern.
  * Contrast narrative B-roll: elderly mom slowly shuffling down a hallway gripping doorframes, contrasted with her stepping smoothly across hardwood floors and outdoors on pavement.
  * Family dynamic: daughter unboxing a second pair from a cardboard parcel on a bed or kitchen counter.

#### 4. Pacing & Editing Direction
* **Pacing:** Quick, conversational, urgent. Fast cuts every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds. Never linger on a static frame.
* **Audio:** Crystal clear iPhone audio with natural room ambiance. No background music during the hook; subtle, low-tempo warm acoustic or lo-fi rhythm tracks entering under dialogue from second 7 onwards, ducked under the voice.
* **On-Screen Captions & Pop-Ups:**
  * Native TikTok/Instagram style bold white text with red/black backing boxes.
  * 2 to 5 words per flash, synced tightly to spoken cadence.
  * Hook opening must feature large centered text with high-contrast color blocks (e.g., white text on black box, key warning words highlighted in bright red).

#### 5. Brand & Legal Compliance Guardrails
* **No Em Dashes:** Strictly zero em dashes in any caption or graphics.
* **Strict Offer Lock:** Use ONLY "Black Friday 60% Off Sitewide" (runs through Dec 5). Do not mention free gifts or bundle offers.
* **Strict Guarantee Lock:** "30-day money-back guarantee" (full refund within 30 days). Do not mention 45 days, store credit, or partial refunds.
* **Shipping & Return Rules:** Do NOT promise free shipping, expedited delivery times, or "easy/hassle-free returns". State only the 30-day money-back guarantee.
* **Medical / Health Claims:** Doctor-recommended and podiatrist-recommended claims are approved. Do NOT claim to cure or treat any disease, do NOT say "orthopedic approved", and never name real medical professionals or fabricate study counts.
* **Social Proof Integrity:** Do NOT use fabricated customer counts or fake star ratings (avoid claims like "4 million walkers" or "2M+ happy customers").

## 2. Hook Options (First 3 to 6 Seconds)

*Note for Creator & Editor: Film all 3 hook options during production so the paid media team can test hook variations against the same winning body script.*

### Hook 1: Direct Transplant (Altruistic Gatekeeper & Warning)
* **Spoken Audio:** "Please do not order too many pairs like I did. Comfort Wear is basically giving these away right now, and I am honestly worried they will sell out for seniors who actually need them."
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):**  
  `[PLEASE DO NOT ORDER TOO MANY PAIRS LIKE I DID]`  
  *(Styling: Heavy bold font, white letters inside a solid red banner at top third of screen)*
* **Visual Direction:**  
  * **0:00 - 0:02:** Extreme close-up of hands pulling on an Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe, sliding heel in smoothly without tying laces, then thumb pressing deep into the bouncy heel cushion.
  * **0:02 - 0:05:** Quick whip-pan to Creator standing in kitchen, holding a second unworn pair fresh out of the parcel box, speaking directly into the phone camera with an earnest, slightly panicked expression.
* **Why It Works:** Pattern-interrupting warning that immediately neutralizes sales resistance. The viewer expects a standard pitch; instead, she hears someone telling her NOT to buy, triggering instant curiosity and perceived scarcity.

### Hook 2: Caregiver Callout (The Mobility & Fall Risk Angle)
* **Spoken Audio:** "If you have an aging mom or you struggle with your balance, please do not buy these shoes unless you actually need them. Comfort Wear dropped their prices 60% for Black Friday, and inventory is running thin."
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):**  
  `[IF YOU HAVE AN AGING MOM... DO NOT BUY THESE YET]`  
  *(Styling: High-contrast white box with black uppercase text, "DO NOT BUY" highlighted in red)*
* **Visual Direction:**  
  * **0:00 - 0:02:** Creator in kitchen looking straight into the camera, looking concerned, pointing to a pair of shoes resting on the counter.
  * **0:02 - 0:05:** Fast B-roll cut: elderly mother in hallway, one hand clutching the doorframe for balance while taking slow, cautious steps in socks, cutting immediately to a shot of the shoe's rugged non-slip grip sole.
* **Why It Works:** Directly targets the adult daughter caregiver and women 55+ experiencing early balance instability. Frames the shoe as vital mobility equipment rather than casual footwear.

### Hook 3: Reverse Scarcity & The Walker Warning
* **Spoken Audio:** "Please stop buying these if your feet feel fine. Comfort Wear just launched their 60% off Black Friday sale, and women who are one slip away from a walker need these before they sell out."
* **On-Screen Text (Overlay):**  
  `[LEAVE THESE FOR WOMEN WHO ACTUALLY NEED THEM]`  
  *(Styling: Bold yellow text on black background, centered over video)*
* **Visual Direction:**  
  * **0:00 - 0:02:** Shock visual: hands holding an Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe, doing the 90-degree flex test to show arch support and torsion resistance.
  * **0:02 - 0:05:** Creator holding up three shoe boxes stacked on her counter, speaking with urgent, authentic delivery: "Stop hoarding them!"
* **Why It Works:** High emotional stakes ("one slip away from a walker") combined with the visual proof of multiple boxes creates intense social proof and fear of missing out.
