# Video Ad Brief: COM468 - Triple Discount

## Strategist Note

- Reference ad: Atria ad m1558114475675781 (15-second offer-explainer voiceover over product).
- Angle: Offer-explainer and Black Friday savings stack. The creative reframes standard e-commerce promotional mechanics into a stacked triple discount to create high perceived value for shoppers seeking pain-relief footwear.
- Target audience: Women ages 50 to 65+, including nurses, retail workers, food service staff, and travelers on their feet all day who suffer from foot pain, plantar fasciitis, and heel fatigue.
- How to produce it:
  - Visual setup: Clean, high-conversion product B-roll paired with bold graphic overlays and checkout UI screen recordings. Feature a female presenter or hands holding and demonstrating the Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe. Highlight key physical details: bending the flexible rocker midsole, showing the stretch-knit upper and wide toe box, pointing to the slip-resistant tread, showing the elastic no-tie laces with pull tab, and showing a selection of the nine colorways.
  - Graphic design: Clean, large-type-friendly typography with strong contrast against product footage, tailored for older female eyes. Keep screen overlays clear and readable on mobile feeds.
  - Voiceover tone: Warm, authentic, conversational female voice reading at a steady, natural pace. The script is calibrated for approximately 15 seconds (under 55 words spoken) without feeling rushed.
- Offer confirmation requirements and policy compliance:
  - Confirmed offer: Black Friday 60% off sitewide running through December 5, plus the standing 30-day money-back guarantee (full refund within 30 days).
  - Unconfirmed elements: Free shipping and additional checkout promo codes are not currently confirmed in store operations. They are clearly noted in the primary script as needing confirmation before launch.
  - Fully confirmed alternative: An alternative 100% confirmed script is included below that swaps unconfirmed store codes for confirmed built-in value (60% off sitewide, free anatomical arch support insoles included, and the risk-free 30-day wear test).
  - Standing constraints: No dollar prices are stated. Only women wear the shoe. All claims remain strictly aligned with verified product features, podiatrist recommendations, and the December 5 deadline.

## Three Hooks

Hook 1 (Direct Offer Transplant)
- Spoken voiceover: Here is how to get our podiatrist-recommended shoes on a triple discount.
- In-scene headline: HOW TO GET A TRIPLE DISCOUNT

Hook 2 (All-Day Foot Pain and Work Focus)
- Spoken voiceover: If your feet ache after being on your feet all day, here is how to stack three discounts on comfort shoes.
- In-scene headline: 3 DISCOUNTS FOR SORE FEET

Hook 3 (Black Friday Urgency and Value Stack)
- Spoken voiceover: Before our Black Friday event ends on December 5, here is how to unlock three discounts in a row.
- In-scene headline: UNLOCK 3 DISCOUNTS BEFORE DEC 5
