## Strategist's Note

- Reference Link: Atria m1789141772364566
- Angle: The 20-Minute Trap vs. The 10,000-Step Mechanical Problem. Most plantar fasciitis sufferers spend months trying fixes that only touch twenty minutes of their day (ice, stretching, night braces, cortisone shots). Meanwhile, their remaining ten thousand steps are spent inside soft foam shoes that compress flat under body weight within hours. Once that soft foam collapses, the arch has zero support, forcing the plantar fascia to absorb the full impact and load of every single step. The problem is mechanical, so the answer must be mechanical: non-collapsing arch support and a rocker sole that takes the pounding off the heel.
- How to Produce It:
  - Cast a relatable female creator (age 50 to 65+) who speaks directly to camera in an authentic, conversational voice. She sounds like an informed friend who is done with gimmicks.
  - Intercut direct-to-camera delivery with clear, tangible visual demonstrations: hands squeezing a squishy foam sneaker flat versus showing the rigid support of the ComfortWear insole, a walking shot showing the foot rolling smoothly over the rocker sole, and a practical demo using the pull tab and elastic no-tie laces.
