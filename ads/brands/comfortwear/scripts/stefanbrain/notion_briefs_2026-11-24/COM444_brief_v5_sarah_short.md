# COM444: Sarah Script (Short & Simple Mike Transplant)

## Hooks with In-Scene Headlines

### Hook 1 (Main Shark Tank Version)
* **Spoken**: This nurse got laughed out of Shark Tank for inventing a shoe that keeps women comfortable on their feet all day.
* **In-scene headline**: Laughed out of Shark Tank for this shoe
* **Visual**: Sarah on Shark Tank as an investor laughs dismissively, cutting to a nurse rubbing her heel, then to the shoe.

### Hook 2 (Conversational Curiosity Hook)
* **Spoken**: What did this nurse invent that made four Shark Tank investors laugh in her face?
* **In-scene headline**: What made the Sharks laugh?
* **Visual**: Sarah standing at the pitch mark, cutting to a running shoe cut in half, then to a foot slipping into ComfortWear.

### Hook 3 (Direct Sneaker Comparison Hook)
* **Spoken**: Shark Tank told this nurse regular sneakers were fine, but now nurses on her floor refuse to wear their Hokas.
* **In-scene headline**: Sharks said regular sneakers were fine
* **Visual**: A nurse tossing worn-out running shoes in the trash and stepping into ComfortWear shoes.

### Alternate Opening (No Shark Tank / Boardroom Rejection)
* **Spoken**: When this nurse showed her new shoe design to big sneaker brands, they literally laughed her out of the room.
* **In-scene headline**: Big shoe brands laughed at her design
* **Visual**: Corporate executives waving dismissively across a table, cutting to Sarah holding her prototype sole.
