### 2. Why The References Work (Direct-Response Teardown)
* **High-Intent Situational Hook:** Calling out major airlines (Delta, American, United) or the familiar misery of flying economy instantly filters for women who have flights booked. It converts passive scrollers into high-attention viewers through immediate situational relevance.
* **The "Travel Day" Cognitive Reframe:** The script shatters the naive assumption that travel is merely "sitting on a plane." It articulates the hidden physical gauntlet: walking miles across unforgiving airport terrazzo, standing 45 minutes in rigid TSA lines, sitting cramped for hours in pressurized air where feet swell, and sprinting across terminals to connecting gates.
* **Systematic Competitor Teardown:** Rather than making vague superiority claims, the script systematically examines popular shoes travelers actually pack (Skechers, Brooks, On Cloud, Hoka) and reveals the specific engineering failure mode of each on a 14-hour travel day. The competitor products are disqualified through objective functional breakdowns: memory foam collapses flat under sustained body weight, narrow runners pinch swollen feet, hollow pods lack arch structure, and oversized plush foam compresses unevenly while destabilizing ankles.
* **Survey Frame Authority:** Integrating our proven COM284 survey mechanism ("We surveyed 500 women over 50 who travel...") establishes immediate objective authority, validating that travel foot agony is universal rather than personal failure.
* **Hero Mechanism as the Exact Antidote:** ComfortWear's Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe is presented as the only shoe engineered specifically for the walk-stand-sit-swell cycle. Dense anatomical arch support prevents arch collapse, the rocker sole rolls through long concourses, the stretch-knit upper flexes as feet swell in flight, and elastic no-tie laces eliminate the TSA struggle.
* **High-Stakes Emotional Arrival Payoff:** Transplants the emotional core from our winning travel campaigns (COM49 "I lost two days of Rome" and the dread of being too exhausted to walk with the grandkids). It frames the shoe as vacation insurance: "their feet didn't hurt. Not hurt less, didn't hurt."

### 3. Production & Creator Guidelines
* **Creator/Narrator:** Relatable woman, age 52 to 62. Warm, authoritative, authentic, and experienced. She sounds like a knowledgeable frequent-flyer friend sharing an insider travel discovery, not a hired spokesperson or actor.
* **Tone & Pacing:** Conversational, spoken, and steady. Keep the rhythm natural and urgent without feeling rushed.
* **Visual Style & B-Roll:**
  * Authentic travel footage: terminal concourses, dragging carry-on suitcases, TSA security bins, cramped economy cabin seating, swollen feet resting under seats, baggage claim carousels.
  * Hands-on shoe testing: side-by-side comparisons on a flat travel table or suitcase. Show hands squeezing squishy foam to show it bottoming out, pointing to narrow toe boxes, flexing the rocker sole, and demonstrating the stretch of the upper.
  * On-feet footage: walking smoothly across hard airport tile, slipping shoes on and off at TSA without tying laces, sitting comfortably with feet uncompressed on the plane.
  * Approved colorways only: show Black Purple, Light Grey, All Black, Grey Pink, or Burgundy.
* **Captions & Text Overlays:** Bold white text with black drop shadow or solid box backing. High contrast, large type, easily readable on mobile screens. No em dashes anywhere in graphics or overlays.
