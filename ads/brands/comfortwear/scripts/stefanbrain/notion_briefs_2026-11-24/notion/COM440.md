### Why This Format Converts (Direct-Response Mechanics)
1. **Unimpeachable Situational Authority:** The presenter is not an influencer, a company spokesperson, or an actor reading a medical script. She is an everyday frontline worker who walks 18,000 steps a day in the exact torture-test environment where the viewer fears failing. Her authority comes from pure observational volume (watching thousands of guests every week).
2. **The Countdown Curiosity Engine:** A reverse countdown ("5 shoes you see on struggling guests") commands high hold rates because the viewer immediately wants to see if the expensive shoes currently sitting in her closet are on the failure list, and which shoe actually takes number one.
3. **The Concrete Foam-Collapse Mechanism:** Plus-sized women are constantly told to buy \$160+ running shoes. Explaining why ultra-soft EVA running foam bottoms out under 200+ lbs provides an immediate "aha" moment that absolves the buyer. Her pain is not because her body is broken, but because running shoes are built for 130 lb runners leaning forward, not a woman carrying 200+ lbs upright on concrete.
4. **Visceral Lost-Moment Stakes:** We anchor directly into the deep emotional pain point from our top winner COM284: the guilt of waving your family ahead while you limp to a concrete bench outside an attraction, holding the backpacks while your grandchildren make core memories without you.
5. **Named-Competitor Price Teardown:** Borrowing the winning structure from COM320, we tear down Hoka (~\$165), On Cloud (~\$180), Brooks (~\$140), and Skechers (~\$90), contrasting their specific structural flaws against ComfortWear's dual-action anatomical arch support and rocker sole.

* **Wardrobe & Compliance Guardrails:** 
  * She must wear a generic theme-park tour-guide outfit: a plaid vest or cardigan over a crisp collared button-down shirt, khaki pants, and a generic plain lanyard.

  * B-roll: Cutaways of tired guests sitting on concrete benches rubbing their heels, POV walking shots of feet on concrete and tram tracks, unboxing and hands-on shoe flex demonstrations, suitcase packing, and clean product feature overlays.
