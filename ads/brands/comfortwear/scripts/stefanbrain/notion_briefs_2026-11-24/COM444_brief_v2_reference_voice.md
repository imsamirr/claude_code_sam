# COM444: Conversational Video Script (Direct Reference Transplant)

## Hooks with In-Scene Headlines

### Hook 1 (Main Shark Tank Version)
* **Spoken**: This woman got laughed out of Shark Tank for inventing a shoe that keeps women comfortable on their feet through twelve-hour shifts.
* **In-scene headline**: Laughed out of Shark Tank for this shoe
* **Visual**: Sarah standing on the Shark Tank stage under studio lights as an investor chuckles dismissively, cutting to a woman in scrubs sitting down and rubbing her heel, then to the ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe resting on a hospital floor.

### Hook 2 (Conversational Curiosity Hook)
* **Spoken**: What did a 52-year-old nurse figure out about twelve-hour shifts that made four Shark Tank investors laugh in her face?
* **In-scene headline**: What made the Sharks laugh at her shoe?
* **Visual**: Sarah standing composed at the pitch mark facing the investors, cutting to an old running sneaker cut in half showing crushed foam, then to a foot stepping easily into a fresh ComfortWear shoe.

### Hook 3 (Direct Sneaker Comparison Hook)
* **Spoken**: Shark Tank told this nurse her shoe idea was unnecessary, but now women on her floor refuse to wear their Hokas.
* **In-scene headline**: Sharks said regular sneakers were fine
* **Visual**: A nurse opening her hospital locker, tossing a pair of worn running shoes into the bin, slipping on ComfortWear shoes with no-tie laces, and walking comfortably down the corridor.

### Alternate Opening (No Shark Tank / Boardroom Rejection)
* **Spoken**: When this nurse showed her new shoe design to executives at two major athletic sneaker brands, they literally laughed her out of the room.
* **In-scene headline**: Big shoe brands laughed at her design
* **Visual**: Two shoe brand executives in suits shaking their heads dismissively across a conference table, cutting to Sarah holding her hand-shaped prototype sole, then to women walking on hard hospital linoleum.
