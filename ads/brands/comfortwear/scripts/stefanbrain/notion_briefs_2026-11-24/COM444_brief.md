# Video Ad Creative Brief: Notion Task COM444

## Strategist's Note

### 1. Reference Ad Breakdown
* **Reference**: ComfoFeet (Atria ad ID `m2098202070816988`, runtime 3:07, viewable at https://app.tryatria.com/ad/m2098202070816988).
* **Format**: Third-person narrated documentary style over dynamic, realistic B-roll and AI-generated footage.
* **Core Direct-Response Skeleton**:
  1. Inciting Rejection / Hook (public humiliation by elite gatekeepers).
  2. The Failed-Fixes Ladder (naming real brands, exact prices, and recurring disappointment).
  3. The Turning Point & The Different Question (shifting blame from the victim to footwear design flaws).
  4. The Prototyping Journey (trial, error, cutting shoes open, testing shapes and densities).
  5. The Breakthrough & Personal Payoff (the shift-proof moment of relief).
  6. Organic Peer Demand (colleagues noticing, begging for pairs).
  7. The Pitch & Rejection (investors laugh off the problem).
  8. Defiance & Brand Genesis (funding production from personal savings).
  9. Product Deep Dive & Hero Mechanism (how it mechanically solves the problem).
  10. Urgent Close & Guarantee (seasonal sale, risk-reversal guarantee, sellout warning).

### 2. Why It Works
* **Validates Cynicism & Names the Enemy**: Sufferers of chronic foot pain have tried everything and feel burned. Naming competitors specifically (Hoka Bondi ~$165, Brooks Ghost ~$140, On Cloud ~$180, Skechers ~$90) and calling out their failure points (running foam compresses in two hours; memory foam flattens in weeks) instantly builds trust. It proves we understand their exact frustration.
* **Reframes the Core Problem**: Shifting the conversation from "which sneaker is the softest" to "why running shoes fail 12-hour shifts." Running shoes are engineered for 45-minute forward jogs on soft asphalt, where squishy foam feels amazing in a store. On a 12-hour concrete shift, that squishy foam collapses, bottoming out so the heel and arch take brutal punishment.
* **Emotional Stakes via Rejection**: Framing the founder as an everyday ER nurse laughed out of Shark Tank creates instant underdog solidarity. Buying the shoe becomes an act of vindication against out-of-touch corporate investors who think working women should just "suck it up."
* **Specific Nurse Proof**: Nurses represent the ultimate litmus test for comfort (~7% of buyers, but the highest-authority voice in the category). When an ad demonstrates surviving "3 12s in a row... 4 12s at the ER with zero pain," it proves comfort for nurses, teachers, retail workers, travelers, and women 50+ alike.

### 3. How to Produce
* **Visual Style**: Fast-paced documentary aesthetic blending realistic UGC footage, authentic hospital B-roll, clean product macro shots, and photorealistic AI-generated scenes (the Shark Tank set, the shoe workshop).
* **Pacing & Cuts**: Rapid visual cuts every 2 to 3 seconds to maintain high retention. Never leave a static talking head or stagnant product shot on screen.
* **On-Screen Text (Captions)**: Bold, clean sans-serif white typography centered or lower-third, with key high-impact words highlighted in bright yellow (e.g., **"laughed off Shark Tank"**, **"$165 Hokas"**, **"dies after 2 hours"**, **"didn't hurt"**, **"60% off sitewide"**). All on-screen text must reinforce the spoken audio without cluttering the screen.
* **Colorways to Feature**: Focus on nurse-approved, high-demand colorways: Black Purple (hero color), Black White, All Black, and White Blue.
* **Voiceover Tone**: Spoken, authentic, grounded, and slightly conversational. It should sound like a friend or colleague telling an incredible true story over coffee, not an announcer pitching a late-night infomercial.

## Hook Options & Alternate Opening

### Hook 1 (Main Shark Tank Version: Situational / Narrative Action)
* **Spoken (Audio)**: "This veteran ER nurse was laughed right off Shark Tank for designing a shoe that keeps nurses completely pain-free through three 12-hour shifts in a row."
* **On-Screen Text**: "Laughed off Shark Tank for this shoe..."
* **Visual**: Dynamic split screen. Left side: a smug investor in a dark suit smirking and waving his hand dismissively on a studio stage. Right side: an exhausted female nurse in navy scrubs sitting on a hospital bench, rubbing her heel with a pained expression. Hard cut to a tight, high-energy hero shot of the ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe in Black Purple bouncing resiliently on concrete.

### Hook 2 (Curiosity / Single-Sentence Open-Ended Hook)
* **Spoken (Audio)**: "What did a 52-year-old nurse discover after three 12-hour shifts that made four Shark Tank investors laugh directly in her face?"
* **On-Screen Text**: "What made the Sharks laugh at her?"
* **Visual**: Cold open on Sarah standing resolute at the pitch mark under intense television studio lighting, facing shadowy investor chairs. Quick cut to a macro B-roll shot of a sneaker sole sliced down the middle, showing flattened, lifeless foam, followed by a glowing anatomical animation of an inflamed foot arch settling into supportive contouring.

### Hook 3 (Aggressive Contrast / Direct Challenge)
* **Spoken (Audio)**: "The Sharks told this floor nurse that big shoe brands already solved foot pain, but today, over half the nurses on her floor refuse to wear their $160 Hokas."
* **On-Screen Text**: "Sharks said Hokas already solved this..."
* **Visual**: A nurse in scrubs walks briskly past hospital lockers, pulls open her locker door, grabs a pair of worn-out Hoka running shoes, and drops them directly into a trash bin. She slips her feet effortlessly into ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoes with elastic no-tie laces, smiles with visible relief, and strides confidently down the hallway.

### Alternate Opening (No Shark Tank / Boardroom Rejection)
* **Spoken (Audio)**: "When this veteran ER nurse showed her new shoe design to executives at two multi-billion-dollar athletic shoe brands, they literally laughed her out of the room."
* **On-Screen Text**: "Big shoe brands laughed at her design..."
* **Visual**: High-angle shot of a sleek glass corporate boardroom. Two male executives in tailored suits lean back, laughing dismissively and shaking their heads. Hard cut to Sarah in hospital scrubs holding a raw, hand-cut sole prototype with unwavering confidence, transitioning immediately into a fast montage of working nurses walking on hard hospital linoleum.
