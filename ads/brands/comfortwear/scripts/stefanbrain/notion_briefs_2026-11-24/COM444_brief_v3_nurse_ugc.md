### Hook 3 (Emotional Reality / The Breaking Point)
* **Spoken**: I was honestly ready to cut my nursing hours because my feet and back ached so badly after three 12-hour shifts, until a charge nurse showed me these.
* **In-scene headline**: Almost cut my nursing shifts over foot pain
* **Visual**: Nurse sitting on her bed after an evening shift, tenderly massaging her heel with a tired expression, cutting quickly to her walking briskly and comfortably down a hospital hallway.
