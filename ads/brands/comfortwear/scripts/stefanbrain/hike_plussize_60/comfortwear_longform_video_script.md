# ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe: Full 6:00 Meta Video Advertorial Script

## Production Overview
* **Asset Name:** ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe - The Two Grandmothers (Longevity & Weight Redistribution Advertorial)
* **Target Audience:** Plus-sized women over 60 (grandmothers, retired women, women on their feet all day dealing with plantar fasciitis, heel pain, afternoon swelling, bunions, and aching knees/back)
* **Format:** Meta Video Ad (Optimized for 9:16 Vertical Reels/Stories and 4:5 Mobile Feed)
* **Total Runtime:** 6 minutes 03 seconds (363 seconds total)
* **Total Shots:** 140 cuts (Average Shot Length: ~2.6 seconds)
* **Narrator:** Dr. Sarah Bennett, DPM (Fictional female podiatric mobility specialist, mid-50s, warm, authoritative, compassionate bedside manner, wearing navy blazer over clinical scrubs with stethoscope/laptop in office)
* **Color Psychology System for On-Screen Text:**
  * **White:** Setup narrative and dialogue.
  * **Yellow Bold All-Caps:** Paradigm reframes, key revelations, core mechanism names.
  * **Red Bold All-Caps:** Pain points, dangerous traditional advice, failure states.
  * **Green Bold All-Caps:** Relief milestones, biological activation, Black Friday offer, 30-day guarantee.
