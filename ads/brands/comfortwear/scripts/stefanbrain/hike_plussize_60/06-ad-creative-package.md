### 7.4 Creative Direction and Production Notes

1. **Talent Casting:**
   * **Narrator (Dr. Sarah Bennett):** Female, Caucasian or ethnically ambiguous, age 50 to 58. Approachable, warm, intelligent, motherly/aunt-like warmth. Professional attire (navy blazer, slate medical scrubs, minimalist silver watch, no gaudy jewelry). Set in a warm, sunlit office with bookshelves, an anatomical foot chart/model, and a laptop.
   * **B-Roll UGC Talent:** Real plus-sized women ages 58 to 72. Diverse ethnicities. Authentic domestic clothing (casual jeans, knit cardigans, comfortable cotton tops). No fitness models, no heavily contoured makeup. Must look like everyday grandmothers, retired nurses, and teachers.
2. **Shoe Props & Colorways:**
   * Focus primarily on the **Classic Black with White Sole** and **All-Black** colorways for versatility and broad appeal, with cutaways to **Cream Beige** and **Heather Gray** for lifestyle variety.
   * Competitor villain shoes must be generic, unbranded archetypes (e.g. ultra-thick neon foam runner with no logos; heavy, stiff black medical shoe with no branding).
3. **Sound Design & Music Bed:**
   * Beats 1 to 5 (0:00 - 2:47): Low, contemplative, slightly somber acoustic guitar or warm cello with subtle ticking clock cadence, emphasizing clinical mystery and emotional gravity.
   * Beats 6 to 9 (2:47 - 4:23): Subtle melodic lift, warm piano and uplifting ambient strings as the solution mechanism and product are revealed.
   * Beats 10 to 15 (4:23 - 6:03): Driving, confident, optimistic acoustic rhythm with warm percussion, propelling the viewer toward the Black Friday call to action without being overly aggressive.
