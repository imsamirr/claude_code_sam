## Strategist's Note

- Context and Heritage: This brief executes Samir's Notion task COM451, centered on the concept "We asked 500 women with Morton's neuroma." With no reference ad provided, it is modeled directly on our top-performing prospecting winner COM284 ("We asked 500 plus-sized women who've been to Disney one simple question," 2.58 ROAS, 153 purchases). It preserves the exact structural skeleton that drove COM284's performance.
- The Angle: Morton's neuroma pain triggers and physical footwear mechanics for women aged 50 to 65+ who spend long hours on their feet. Survey data from 251 post-purchase respondents shows their primary sensations are burning, tingling, numbness, and the persistent feeling of a pebble or bunched-up sock under the ball of the foot. The script exposes why typical comfort footwear fails: narrow toe boxes squeeze the forefoot bones together and pinch the intermetatarsal nerve, while soft foam soles collapse under weight, forcing the inflamed ball of the foot to take the brunt of every step. On the ComfortWear side, the copy describes strictly physical shoe benefits without making medical or nerve claims: a wide stretch-knit toe box that gives toes room to spread instead of being squeezed together, a podiatrist-recommended anatomical arch support insole that holds its shape all day, and a rocker midsole that rolls the foot smoothly through each step so the forefoot is never jammed into the ground.
- Production and Talent Direction:
  - On-camera Talent: Woman aged 50 to 65+, warm, authentic, natural, and relatable. She should speak like a real peer sharing an honest discovery, using natural contractions and conversational pacing rather than polished ad-speak. Everyday casual wardrobe (knit sweater or top with comfortable pants).
  - Environment: Real home setting, ideally a bright kitchen, dining room, or hallway with hard flooring (tile, hardwood, or laminate). Natural daylight, shot on a smartphone (handheld or tabletop tripod) to preserve an authentic UGC feel.
  - Camera Work and B-roll: Direct-to-camera dialogue intercut with tight, clear B-roll demonstrating key physical shoe features. Visual cutaways include stretching the knit upper to showcase toe room, pressing firmly on the insole arch to prove it does not collapse, using the pull tab at the heel to slide the shoe on comfortably (avoiding any touchless or step-in entry implication), and low-angle floor shots demonstrating the smooth rocking motion while walking across hard floors.
  - Text Graphics: In-scene headline overlays appear exclusively on the hook row, the competitor teardown rows, and the offer row. All other visual cuts remain clean.
