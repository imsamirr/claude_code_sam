# Video Ad Brief: COM448 (ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoe)

## Overview
- Project: ComfortWear (comfortorthowear.com)
- Task ID: COM448
- Concept: "3 reasons a 250lbs woman can finally attend Disney"
- Target Audience: Women over 200 to 250+ pounds, ages 45 to 65+, planning or taking high-step family vacations (theme parks, cruises, walking tours). They struggle with plantar fasciitis, aching arches, heel spurs, bunions, and swollen feet, and dread being forced to sit out while their families enjoy the day.

## Strategist's Note

### Reference Ad
- Reference link: Atria ad m29150269954622867 (first-person direct-to-camera testimonial).
- Reference premise: "Three reasons a 290 pound man can finally walk steady again at 68."

### The Angle
This script adapts the high-converting Atria direct-response structure to our validated plus-size Disney audience, originally proven by top performer COM284 ("We asked 500 plus-sized women who've been to Disney...", 2.58 ROAS). 

The emotional tension centers on the heartbreak of being left behind. Carrying 200 to 250+ pounds for 15,000 to 20,000 steps on concrete turns a dream family vacation into physical agony. The narrator addresses the real problem directly: popular "cloud" running shoes fail heavier bodies because soft EVA foam quickly squashes flat, causing the arches to collapse inward and sending shockwaves directly into the heels and knees.

Instead of generic shoe advice from people who do not walk in a heavier body, ComfortWear solves the root mechanical issue. The podiatrist-recommended anatomical arch support insole maintains its structure under real weight, while the rocker midsole absorbs impact and propels the foot forward.

### Production and Casting Direction
- Creator: A real woman, roughly 220 to 260 pounds, between 48 and 62 years old. She should look like a genuine, relatable mom or grandmother with a warm, conversational, unpolished screen presence.
- Wardrobe: Everyday casual travel clothes, such as a comfortable t-shirt, jeans or capris, and a simple crossbody bag. She must not look like a Disney cast member (no navy/white polos, no nametags, no theme park uniforms).
- Filming Environment: A bright, authentic home setting (kitchen island packing a park day bag or a living room entryway) with natural daylight. Quick b-roll cutaways can show her walking comfortably outside or demonstrating the shoe features up close.
- Tone and Delivery: Confident, empathetic, and spoken like honest advice shared between friends. Use natural contractions and an easy conversational pace.
- Key Product Handling: The creator holds a clean pair of ComfortWear Ortho Stretch Cushion Shoes. She highlights the firm arch insole, bends the rocker sole to show the smooth roll, stretches the knit toe box to show room for bunions, and demonstrates the elastic no-tie laces with the pull tab for easy slip-on wear.
