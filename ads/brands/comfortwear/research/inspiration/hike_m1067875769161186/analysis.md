## 4. Why it's #1 for 60+

1. **It sells independence, not shoes.** "Strong, steady, independent into their 90s" vs "fading in their 60s".
2. **It absolves the body.** "Assumed it was age. It wasn't." The fix is a purchase, not willpower.
3. **The reframe turns past failures into proof.** Everyone 60+ has a closet of "supportive" shoes that didn't work.
4. **The mechanism is learnable in one analogy** (the cast).
5. **No effort is asked** ("you already walk every day").
6. **It's long enough to be an advertorial,** with two CTAs and an FAQ, so the click is pre-sold.

## 5. What we can't borrow

| Hike element | Why not for us | Our substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Mortality parable ("one had passed away"), "double the risk of dying" | Medical/fear claims; no cure or outcome claims | Life-moment stakes: one still walks her granddaughter to the bus stop, the other stopped going places she can't park by the door |
| Research stats (34,000 adults, 10-second test) | No invented or unverifiable numbers; mortality framing | The podiatrist's observation + **our own self-test** the viewer can do tonight (see §7) |

## 6. The flip: our mechanism for plus-size women over 60

**Hike says:** support and cushion put the foot to sleep, so go thin and flat.
**We say:** *that advice was written for a lighter, younger body.* For a heavier woman over 60:
1. **The heel's own cushion thins with age** (the natural fat pad under the heel). A thin barefoot sole + more body weight = the heel takes every step alone.
2. **Soft foam flattens under more weight,** usually by midday. "Soft isn't the same as supportive." (Our proven COM258 line; also YouTube: "loads of cushioning… make my feet feel more tired".)
3. **Feet widen and swell** through the day, and most "wide" shoes still squeeze ("feel like vices", Reddit and our databank).
4. **Bending to tie laces is a real barrier** (Reddit: "can't get his shoes on due to his swollen feet", "difficulty getting them on and tying the laces").

**Reframe line (our "more support was the cause"):** *"It was never your weight. And it was never your age. It's that every shoe you've tried was built for someone else's body."* This echoes our proven "stop taking shoe advice from people who don't walk in your body" (COM284, the current 2.58 ROAS plus-size winner).

**Product truth to hit:** arch support + cushioned heel that holds its shape · stretch-knit upper and roomy toe box that gives when feet swell · no-tie elastic laces + pull tab (step in, no bending) · slip-resistant grip · nine colors (not "orthopedic-looking").
**Objection to pre-empt:** the rocker sole ("feels different the first day") and stability (YouTube/Reddit: rocker and "bouncy" soles scare older buyers). Say **"wide, steady base"**, never "bouncy" or "cloud".
