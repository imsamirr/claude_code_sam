## 6. Net-new long-form ideas for NEXT week

These are evidence-backed ideas, not copy. StefanBrain writes every word. Each idea avoids LF05–LF14 and MS1–MS8.

1. **The observer camera or log: a son or daughter discovers why Mom, 66+, still working, sits in the car.**
   - **Persona:** a 40–50 adult child as narrator and gift buyer, watching a 65+ mother who works on her feet (stadium courtesy staff, a big-box cashier, school lunch).
   - **Device:** a doorbell or driveway camera, or the step counter on her phone. The footage becomes the before-and-after log, which keeps any change observed rather than claimed.
   - **Evidence:**
     - Cuurt m1921030765812313: same category, 13 days, active.
     - Nerve Support m29780612371559067 and Janice Myers m1142935804758430: both rank 1 with an adult-child narrator.
     - Feedback: the 65+ stadium courtesy worker (5★) and the 65+ lunch lady who says "Take my shoes off".
   - **Fits:** Christmas gifting.
   - **Difference from LF12:** in LF12 a mother watches her CNA daughter. Here the child watches a 65+ parent.
2. **Family planner vs. one pair: a Disney or Europe packing fight.**
   - **Premise:** a relative's itinerary or packing list says to bring three pairs. The narrator, 50+, brings one pair and the planner's feet give out first.
   - **Evidence:**
     - Nimbao m1297234525895064: **same category and same persona, 126 days**, still active.
     - Feedback: "Needed support for Disney vacation" (nanny, 65+, 5★), plus 3 Europe trip rows.
     - Reddit: "almost 70 year old parents for a week at Disney… VERY vivid memories of many shoe fiascos".
   - **Difference from MS6:** MS6 is about being left off the trip. This is an artifact (the list) plus a one-pair proof.
   - **Guardrails:** no delivery-speed beats. Don't give the shoes an overnight arrival like Nimbao does, because we can't make shipping claims.
3. **The young nurse who resented the 74-year-old: told from the new grad's side.**
   - **Premise:** a 26–30 nurse resents the oldest nurse on the unit for being slow. She learns what the older nurse hides at hour six, and the story ends with the older nurse outlasting her.
   - **Evidence:**
     - Reddit: r/nursing 1ims3er (1,807 upvotes) and the 1lbbucl late-70s LPN (1,339 upvotes).
     - Orthora m1643871270551897: the 52-year-old nurse vs the 24-year-old new grad, rising +47 places.
     - Feedback: nurse 65+, 5★.
   - **Persona:** older nurses, 50–65 and 65+. They are the buyers; the narrator is the young nurse.
   - **Difference from LF09:** LF09 is told by the older nurse; this narrator and point of view are new.
4. **"I left the 1-star review": honest reversal after a sizing mistake.**
   - **Premise:** a 55–65 woman orders her usual size, gets it wrong, and writes an angry review. She exchanges, gets the right fit, and goes back to edit the review.
   - **Evidence:**
     - Hook pattern: Joan Reeves m4077364819065898 (saved, 13 days) and Barbara/Snatched (39 days, in the earlier bank).
     - Feedback: sizing is the #3 objection (good fit 39.7%), and the newest rows have "too big", "too small" and "can't get my foot in". The 2026-11-12 deep dive found honest 5★ voices admit a flaw.
   - **Guardrails:** handle the first-wear tightness truthfully. Only use the approved 30-day money-back guarantee. Never say "easy exchanges" or imply a complaint was wrong.
5. **Two jobs in December: the cashier on two concrete floors.**
   - **Premise:** a 55–65 woman works a hardware-store register by day and a grocery register by night through the holidays to pay for Christmas.
   - **Evidence:**
     - Feedback (5★): "Head Cashier for HOMEDEPOT and cashier for Kroger … concrete floor for 8+ hours for 5-6 a week".
     - Feedback: "Retail merchandiser… cement for 10 hours… Buy expensive shoes every 3 months".
     - Reddit r/AmazonFC peak threads (11-05): "5 days of 11hrs duty".
     - Orthora's 11-15 "limping to your car" callout series shows the shift-worker moment is being bought right now.
   - **Difference from LF14:** LF14 is a warehouse; this is retail registers and two employers, a new setting.
