## Cross-ad patterns that differ from our playbook
1. **The brand comes very late or never.**
   - Koprez never names its brand (the product is revealed as "the sleeve has straps").
   - The Orthora blog page holds the brand for an "EDIT: since people are asking" at ~90%.
   - The Kirsten 9k body names it only in the URL.
   - The early-brand ads are the exceptions: Denise 50%, Oobend 40%, Hike 34%.
   - Our "brand at ~60% in one short line" rule is too rigid. An unnamed product with criteria first, then a Reddit-style EDIT reveal, is a format we can copy cleanly.
2. **The shared enemy is "it's not inflammation/age, it's degeneration/foam".** Koprez, Sculptara and Orthora all use a phase-shift mechanism: after 4 weeks it is breakdown, not swelling; by hour 6 the foam is flat. Then every failed fix is re-read with a refrain ("Built for swelling"). Orthora adds a **physical self-test (the thumb press on the heel)** as both the discovery and the CTA. That is directly usable for Comfort Wear, and it is a clean type-14 close.
3. **Nurse-specific shame and vindication beats.** Kid drawings, frozen peas as dinner, a manager offering a desk job, a review with a rival's input, "you're not limping". Peer nurses are the messengers. The early results are reframed: "first shift I didn't notice anything, which was the point" and "sore isn't broken". This is a better twist on "week one, nothing".
4. **Video long-form is booming in this category.** 300–600s voice-over stories, often as **3D cartoons** (Orthora 429s, Oobend 305–428s): revenge, travel, a foreign or local messenger. None are scrolling-text. Oobend and Hike each launched hundreds of these last week. Orthora's 306s Barcelona story has run 52 days and hit rank 1.
5. **Hook iteration at scale on fresh pages.** Sculptara runs 2 bodies × 3 hooks; Denise runs 1 template × 5 locations; Koprez runs 7 duplicates. Several of these "personas" are recycled pages: Kirsten was a vet in July, Denise sold GLP-1 hair and reflux products, Hike posts as "Senior Times Daily". Fabricated doctor personas remain a compliance risk we should not copy. The **senior-home / knee angle died within a day for Orthora**, and long-copy statics never lasted for Stepprs (≤2 days) or Hike (≤38 days).
