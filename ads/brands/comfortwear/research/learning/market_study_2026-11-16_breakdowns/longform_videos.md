# C — Long-form VIDEO study (scroll-text + story-led), found 2026-11-16

Method: get_library_ad + free transcript for every ad. I pulled frames from the CDN mp4s with ffmpeg (at 0/25/50/85–97% of runtime) to confirm the format, and ran volumedetect plus the empty-transcript check to tell voice from music. Char positions for the very long bodies are estimates (±3 pts) from section landmarks, cross-checked against which text was on screen at 25/50/85% of runtime. Words/sec for the scroll videos ≈ (chars ÷ 5.7) ÷ seconds. For the voiced videos it is transcript words ÷ seconds.
Frames are in `(frames kept in the session scratchpad, not committed)`.

Paid transcriptions: 1 confirmed (m1665310724979818, which came back empty: music only). I also called it twice for m1683044290143367 (Linda Carey). Both calls timed out and no transcript exists, so at most 1 extra credit was spent.

## Cross-ad patterns that differ from our scroll-video notes and playbook
1. **Scroll speed and length:** the winning scroll videos run **~4–4.5 wps** (HHF 4.0–4.5, Naturva 4.3), not 2.3–2.5, and they are **8–19 min / 1,900–5,100 words**, not 4–5 min / 600–720 words. Nobody can read 4.5 wps on mobile, so the video works as a "skim the wall of text + read the primary text" unit. The only slow one (Emma Livy at 1.5 wps) has been retired.
2. **The caption is the full story.** Both scroll advertisers put the ENTIRE 11–29k body in the primary text as well. Our notes say "caption 1–3 sentences".
3. **Proven-static → scroll-video pipeline.** Each scroll video is a 1:1 re-cut of a static that has already run 2–4 weeks. Same text, music only, no voice. It's cheap and it extends a winner's life (HHF's 11k story is #1 of 551 at 106 days as a video).
4. **Background is often the ingredient or the narrator's world, not the hook object.** HHF uses the same black-garlic bowl for every story. Naturva shows a spirometry test. Emma Livy used the hook object (a bruised belly). Our "hook object" rule isn't necessary to scale. Music-only beats voice-over in every scroll winner.
