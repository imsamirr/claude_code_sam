## 3. Formats, hooks and open loops worth borrowing (formats only)

1. **The outlier who shouldn't be fine.** Cuurt's front desk (an older regular who walks out fresh) and Oobend's twin (same genes, different shoes). The open loop is "why is she OK and I'm not?", and it closes around 33–40% with the shoes. It fits a nurse or teacher who notices one older colleague, and it echoes Reddit's "coworkers have plans after the shift, I go home stiff". Cuurt's version is surviving.
2. **The borrowed or found unbranded pair.** Cuurt's resort loaner (37 d, top 1% of its pool) and house manager (42 d). The product turns up as an object with no logo, so curiosity carries the story to 40–58% before any pitch. For us this could be a holiday-trip loaner, or a pair left behind by a guest or relative. Brief StefanBrain on the format only.
3. **Adult daughter + mother's emergency + the expert who missed it.** Sculptara (podiatrist daughter, 2 AM bathroom fall) and Balmve (bathtub, 911) launched the same frame in the same week. It is a strong buyer-for-mom frame and matches feedback angle A2. **Our claims rules block fall prevention and invented doctors.** The borrowable part is the scene (night trip, holding the furniture, "she's doing well for her age") with a modest, non-medical payoff.
4. **Husband (or partner) POV about a quiet withdrawal.** Cuurt's two 40-day runners show slow shrinking ("she quietly quit", "she stopped riding with me") with brand names at 75–90%. The same withdrawal works for a wife skipping Christmas shopping trips or Disney days. It is new for us; we have no husband-narrator long-form yet.
5. **Holiday-trip stories with a natural before/after.**
   - Orthora's Barcelona video opens with a stranger's verdict and a friend auditing the packed foot kit.
   - Noverly uses the same sunbed one year later.
   - Reddit backs this up with "one pair of boots for the whole Christmas-market trip". Two customers this week bought for a cruise or Disney.

   The format is **suitcase audit → trip → the same place a year later**.

Also noted:
- **Receipts tally** (Nourial $7,760; Oobend Hoka $170 + orthotics $400). Our survey has "Skechers every 3 months" and "Hokas at $160".
- **Self-test P.S.** (Cuurt's insole stand test, Auniva's mirror check).
- **Dramatization disclaimer** at the end of every Cuurt story ("built from real messages customers sent us; names changed"). Worth raising for compliance.
- **Guarantee as the hook** (SmoothSpine: "return label printed before I opened it").

**Avoid:**
- profanity "expert rants" (Auniva, Cuurt's surgeon): off-brand for us
- naming rival brands as villains (ComfoFeet, Orthora, Sculptara)
- 20k+ bodies in the shoe category (all four Sculptara bodies died within 7 days)
- divorce and revenge frames (HIKE, Whispore): unproven, and the HIKE video is only at 218/1,967
