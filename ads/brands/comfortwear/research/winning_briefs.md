**1. COM49 — Teacher Traveling Yapper (🏆 WINNER, May)**: https://app.notion.com/p/36723caff44480638848f948e361372a
- Audience: teacher in her 50s, Europe travel (Rome/Portugal). UGC, filmed on the couch with the shoes close to camera.
- Pain: arches "done" by day 3 of the trip, lost two days of vacation sitting on the hotel balcony.
- Hooks: "Please don't wear your Skechers to Europe. I'm begging you." / "If you're a teacher in your 50s and you're planning any kind of trip this year, listen to me before you pack the shoes you wear to school." / "Hands down the best shoes if you're going on a trip with a lot of walking but you still want to look put together. And this isn't an ad…"
- Beats: credential (teacher, on feet all day) → failure story (Rome) → switch (Portugal, 18k steps on cobblestone/hills, feet fine) → style proof (dresses, linen, shorts, "didn't even pack a second pair") → mechanism (wide toe box, arch that "doesn't squish flat", stretch upper, "built for nurses") → offer.
- Objections: style/"put together"; buying online; price. Proof: step count, built-for-nurses. Offer: 40% off, under $60, 45-day money-back. CTA: "Link's in my bio."
