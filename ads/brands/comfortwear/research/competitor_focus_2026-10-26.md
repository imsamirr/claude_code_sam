- **Viasox:** "Foot & Ankle Swelling? Start Here" (one headline across a whole catalog of bundles = easy test), "Buy 2, Get 3 FREE: 5 pairs for the price of 2", "Bundle of the Week", color/pattern drops ("New Autumn Knit just dropped"), customer voice "Y'all, at 62 my feet puff up and are always swollen... first sock that actually helped" (62-year-old woman, direct, folksy).

Use for the "BUY 2 PAIRS, GET 2 FREE GIFTS" concepts: show the free items (socks pack, travel bag) clearly, counted, with a caution-tape or "limited" device. Viasox's swelling headline is a validated hook for our swollen-feet concepts. Hollow's "pay for X, take home Y" math line works in the either/or BF offers (do not mix with 60% sitewide).

## What to do with this (nurse + travel + Black Friday)
1. **Scarcity stamp on a clean hero shoe** ("Back in stock" / "Sold out 3x") only if true; otherwise use the neutral "Black Friday" tag.
2. **Occupation identity headline + 3 pills** ("The shoe a nurse can stand in").
3. **Big-number-on-shoe static** ("X% of nurses switched from running shoes" only if we can substantiate; else use step/hour numbers).
4. **Nurse vocabulary additions from Clove:** messy stuff, easy clean, back to nursing school, every shift, rounds; **WearStand:** recovery, 12+ hours standing, shift after shift.
5. **Gift-with-purchase and "pay for 2, take home 4" math** for Black Friday offer B.
6. **First-person result quote about someone else** (Hike: "My mom stopped falling") for gift_for_her concepts, without fall/medical claims.
