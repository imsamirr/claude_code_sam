## Learnings logged in the old tracker

- **Winner:** COM251 / "203_Iteration_UGC… healthcareworkerstop_purplegradient_7reasonswhy_EarlyQualification". This is the 203 UGC that is still the best-ROAS video (3.05).
- **Hooks that stop the scroll** (hook rate 44–64%): "3 reasons I almost quit nursing", "Don't buy this" (47%), lymphedema specialist (57% hook, 26% hold), senior falling (58%), feet wrecked from travel (64%), massage-gun "best way to finish a 12-hr shift" (53%).
- **What killed concepts:** generic podiatrist-rates or tier-list formats with no ComfortWear payoff ("no performance at all"), too much on-screen text (COM624, the 12-hour-shift static turned into video), stock footage, slow pace, copy/creative age mismatch (copy says 70, creator is 40), and "missing the real problems, only have the product" (COM560).
- **Landing-page issues** flagged 4 times (COM598, COM544, COM515, COM541): good hook/hold but no sales. Worth checking before blaming the creative.
- **Low-spend but strong metrics:** Caregiver / fall prevention (COM586), senior mashups (COM574), retirement travel (COM580).
