## Strategic notes for scripts
- **Before → after:** "kick them off after my shift" / "arches incredibly sore" / "feet cramp" → "don't hurt at the end of a long shift" / "walking on pillows" / "pain free."
- **Failed solutions buyers mention:** cheap knit slip-ons that "feel like a slipper"; Skechers with "absolutely no arch support" and "too narrow"; buying aftermarket insoles; $140–$170 premium shoes that "came unglued", "fell apart in 4 months", "stiff", "squeak"; Tylenol.
- **Objections to answer before they come up:** (1) "Will it fit my wide or swollen foot or bunion?" Answer with the stretch knit and toe room. (2) "Will my heel slip out of a sock shoe?" Answer with the bungee toggle lock. (3) "Is the support real or just foam?" Show the anatomical insole. (4) "Does it look orthopedic or like an old lady shoe?" Show it styled. (5) "Will it fall apart?" Answer with the guarantee. (6) "Break-in or tilt?" Say comfortable from day one, rocker eases the step (note STQ "took about a week to get used to the added height and tilt").
- **Audience signals in the reviews:** nurses/12-hour shifts, OR staff, teachers, retail, warehouse, servers, baristas, Chipotle, school/hospital walking; Disney, D.C., Iceland, Scotland cobblestones, airports; buyers shopping for "my mom", "my 85 yr old mother", "94 year old mom"; seniors with bunions and hammertoes, knee surgery, spinal surgery, ankle reconstruction.
