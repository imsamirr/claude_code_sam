**Collected:** 2026-11-18. I used web_search to find the videos, then scrape_social_comments to pull comments sorted by relevance, with at most one reply per thread.
**Volume:** 676 comments from 26 videos. 24 videos returned comments: 1 failed to scrape, 1 had comments turned off, and several returned only 1 to 3 comments. About 470 comments were substantive (25+ characters, not from the video's creator).
**Rule:** Every quote is verbatim, typos and emoji included. Ellipses (…) mark trims of long comments. The tag `[video_id | likes]` gives the source; see Section 1.

> **Caveats.** (a) YouTube commenters skew more male and more runner-heavy than ComfortWear's buyer. Age and gender come only from what commenters say about themselves, such as "I'm 62" or "I'm 79". (b) Disney coverage is **thin**. Disney shoe videos on YouTube have very few comments (1–15 each), so most of the "Disney/travel" language below comes from Europe-travel and theme-park-adjacent comments. ## 6. Quick takeaways for scripting
- **The morning first step and the end-of-shift collapse** are the two most-repeated moments. A third, "hour 5+ of standing / day 3 of the trip", gives a strong travel angle.
- **Big-brand disappointment is common.** Hoka, Oofos and max-cushion running shoes draw as many complaints as praise. "I tried Hoka and still hurt" is a usable switch narrative, but don't claim to be "softer than Hoka". Commenters are already skeptical of too-soft, unstable shoes.
- **Price matters.** Many commenters say $100–$150+ shoes are out of reach. At $59.95, ComfortWear can credibly use "the comfort shoe you don't have to save up for".
- **Slip-on and no-tie demand is real:** silicone no-tie laces at airports, Kizik ("no strings to tie"), and "he prefers a slip on". Bungee laces answer this, and they also answer the high-instep Velcro complaint.
- **Watch-outs:** the rocker midsole may draw the "the sole naturally let's your foot rock … stability isn't the best" objection, and the knit upper may draw "big toe poked through the top fabric". Prepare proof (stable standing demo, durability UGC).
