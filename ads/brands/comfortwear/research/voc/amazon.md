## 2. Why buyers chose them (as stated in reviews)
1. **A specific pain they could not walk through**: plantar fasciitis, bunions, neuropathy, knee or back problems. "I've been searching for a suitable sneaker to fit my bunion" (B0C76LZW6K).
2. **A recommendation from a podiatrist or doctor** (Hoka, Brooks, Vionic, NB 928). "They were recommended by my foot doctor" (B0D5FVDNS6).
3. **Not having to bend over**: hands-free and slip-on matter to older buyers and anyone recovering from knee or ankle surgery. "I have a hard time bending over" (B0C76LZW6K).
4. **A coworker had them**: "I bough these because I seen my co-worker wear them" (B07NP55W1Z). In Treesky reviews, coworkers ask where to buy them.
5. **Rebuying a trusted model**: "my 10th pair of Brooks", "wearing this style for almost 20 years" (NB 928), "Got this to replace my 5 yr old navy Skechers".
6. **A cheaper version of a known brand**: "half the price of sketchers!" (B07NP55W1Z); "Same as Golden Goose but at 1/10 the cost" (B0GXNF5NT4).
7. **A job or a trip coming up**: 12-hour shifts, a kitchen floor, Italy, Disney, Iceland.

## 5. Complaints, objections and return reasons (ranked)
`[KNIT]` marks complaints that also show up on the knit look-alikes and so apply directly to our shoe type.

1. **Sizing is inconsistent and causes most returns** `[KNIT]`. Fit is the most negative or mixed theme on almost every listing (STQ fit -2,702; Go Walk Joy size -1,198 and fit -2,495; Crocs fit -896 and size -315). Buyers report "runs large" and "runs small" for the same shoe. No half sizes (STUNAHOME, Orthora, Crocs). EU size confusion: "it's a size 38, what is that?" (STUNAHOME), "I received a size 42 not US" (Goferlaa). Size charts are wrong (JollyPetal).
2. **No real arch support despite "orthopedic" claims** `[KNIT]`. STQ -619, Go Walk Joy -423, STUNAHOME -101, Goferlaa "Lack of arch support" +3 / -23, JollyPetal +1 / -11, Orthora +1 / -8. On cheap knit shoes this is the strongest betrayal theme.
3. **Falls apart within weeks or months** `[KNIT]`: soles peel, heel lining breaks down, holes at the flex point. Rykä -527, Vionic -141, STUNAHOME -113, OOFOS +5 / -34; also JollyPetal, LOCFIR, Treesky, Skechers Arcata. Usual timeline: "2 months", "3 months", "6th time wearing them".
4. **Hard to get on: the heel collapses and the upper "is like a sock"** `[KNIT]`. STUNAHOME ease of use +44 / -116 and STQ +545 / -711. This hits the stretch-knit format specifically.
5. **Feels fine at first, then hurts a few hours into a shift** `[KNIT]`: "comfortable at first... hurt after just 3 hours" (STUNAHOME), "5 hours into my shift", "within the first 3 hours" (Orthora), "doesn't make it thru a 12 hr shift" (OOFOS).
6. **Not actually slip resistant** `[KNIT]`. STQ slip resistance +272 / -688 ("you will go sliding"); Scurtain "not as advertised"; Go Walk Joy "dangerously slippery on hardwood or vinyl". Kitchen and restaurant workers check this claim the hardest.
7. **Heel slips out or the foot slides forward** `[KNIT]`: STQ, Scurtain, ITAZERO "back of the shoe a bit low", Goferlaa "wouldn't stay on", JollyPetal "By the end of day 2 very loose", Skechers Arcata.
8. **Narrow toe box despite wide or bunion claims**: Coral "For all the hype about being good for bunions... toes smashed", Skechers Flex "pinky toes get pinched", Treesky "fairly pointed".
9. **Squeaking**: Orthofeet Kita squeakiness +6 / -89 (the arch inserts), Coral, Go Walk Joy soles, Crocs "squeaks... when your feet start sweating", STUNAHOME grey pair, OOFOS "sticky" sound.
10. **Odor**: Go Walk Joy "STINK so bad after only a few wears", Coral "weird odor", Scurtain "very strong smell", Rykä "smelled after walking an hour".
11. **Price versus how long it lasts** (premium and mid tier): Vionic "Not the quality you would hope for $120", OOFOS "don't seem durable for the price", Kita "overpriced".
12. **Stretches out or packs down**: Go Walk Joy "stretched out after only 4 to 5 wearing", JollyPetal looser by day 2, Rykä "cushioning began to sink".
13. **Misc.**: water soaks straight through knit (STQ "A drop of water will instantly soak them"), insert slides out (JollyPetal, Goferlaa), laces too long or too short (Coral, Brooks, Arcata), heel collar rubs (Coral, Hoka), "clunky... heavy" (Kita), "Super ugly" (Brooks).

## 6. What knit look-alikes get criticized for (our biggest risk)
These come from STQ, STUNAHOME, Goferlaa, JollyPetal, Scurtain, ITAZERO, LOCFIR and Orthora, which use the same format and claims we do.

1. **"Orthopedic" in name only.** Buyers are angry when a shoe sold as orthopedic is flat: "Absolutely no arch support or anything orthopedic about them" (B0GXNF5NT4); "supposed to be orthopedic, but there is NO support" (B0G5KQ2JKK); "Not as advertised" (B0H7784JHX). Orthora makes the same claims we make at $55.99 and sits at 2.6 stars with 47% 1-star reviews.
2. **Fine for an hour, fails at hour 3 to 5.** The shift is the test buyers apply, and these shoes fail it: "Feet hurt worse with these shoes after a 12 hour shift" (B0CRBGBB4Y); "Not for 2 hour shift work let alone 12 hrs. Total ripoff" (B0H7784JHX).
3. **A sock-like upper with a soft heel makes the shoe hard to get on.** "you fight to get your foot in the back", "No stiffness at all", "Long shoe horn helps". This is the opposite of what a slip-on promises an older buyer.
4. **Thin insole and a sole you can feel the floor through**: "The sole is paper thin", "I can still feel the hard sole", "insole already came out".
5. **Durability at 1 to 3 months**: "Fell apart not even after 2 and a half months", "sole split from the back heel fabric. Bought in June 3rd threw away Sept 11th".
6. **Sizing chaos**: EU and US mix-ups, no half sizes, "too short and too wide", "tried 3 different sizes and none fit".
7. **Heel slip-off and foot sliding**, because the knit gives and stretches over the day.
8. **Slip-resistant claims fail in kitchens**: "Do not buy these if you are in the food industry" (B07NP55W1Z).
9. **Seen as cheap or dropshipped**: "Cheap not comfortable same shoes on Shein" (B0GXNF5NT4). Goferlaa and JollyPetal use identical bullet copy.
10. **Looks are not the problem.** Knit look-alikes score very well on appearance (STUNAHOME +139 / -12, Goferlaa +45 / -3, JollyPetal +22 / -0). Buyers like how they look and then return them over support, fit and durability.

**What the well-rated knit shoes do differently** (ITAZERO 4.3, Scurtain 4.2, Orthofeet Coral 4.1 at 6k reviews): reviewers mention a real arch insole, "adjustable" fit through a strap or laces, the insole being replaceable ("I can replace insoles with ones... recommended by podiatrist"), and sizing advice in the listing ("for narrow feet, we recommend sizing down by one full size"; Crocs: "Runs large—size down if between sizes").

## 7. Per-persona notes

**Nurses and 12-hour shifts**
- Shift length is how they judge a shoe, and they will name the hour it failed.
- They praise: no pain after a 10 to 12 hour shift, standing on concrete, support that lasts the whole shift.
- They reject: soft foam that "doesn't make it thru a 12 hr shift" (OOFOS), "As a nurse who stands 12 plus hours a day. Not the shoe for you" (Rykä), "I work in the medical field and they were worthless" (Coral).
- Wins: Go Walk Joy ("nurse... 12 hour shifts... all the support I need"), Hoka ("12hr shifts and these leave my feet feeling good"), STQ ("medical professional who is on their feet for 12 hours").
- Crocs: an OR nurse notes squeak once feet sweat.

**Restaurant, retail and kitchen workers**
- Slip resistance is a hard requirement, and they test it on wet and greasy floors.
- Value matters: "Worth the money tbh if you can't afford shoes for crews" (Treesky).
- Word of mouth from coworkers drives purchases.
- Tread wears smooth fast: "soles have completely lost there tread after just 2 months" (Skechers Arcata, a waitress).
- Some prefer laces to slip-ins for security: "I love the fact that they are lace up and not slip ins" (Treesky).

**Women 50 to 65+ and retirees**
- Not bending to tie laces is a major driver: "Not for a senior! not able to bend over to tie the shoe laces" (Coral, a negative); "I have a hard time bending over" (Skechers Flex).
- Balance and stability come up (line dancing, a 94-year-old mom, knee surgery).
- They do not want to look old: "don't look like 'old lady shoes'" (Kita summary) versus "the pointy toe makes them look old lady" (Arcata).
- Rebuying in "every color" is common (Kita, Coral, Arcata).
- Bought for or by family: "Bought for my 80 year mom", "my 94 year old mom".

**Travelers (Disney, Europe)**
- Name-checked trips: Italy (Vionic; Goferlaa "with insoles"), Greenland and Iceland gravel trails (Kita), Disney World (Brooks, rotated two pairs), New Orleans (NB 928), D.C. school trip (Brooks), airports (Scurtain), the zoo (Arcata).
- Objections: "flex a lot, so not great for uneven surfaces, like cobblestones" (Kita); "ZERO... ankle support... trip of 5 days of short hikes" (STUNAHOME).
- They measure success in steps and miles: "28,000 steps (over ten miles)".

**Plantar fasciitis and heel pain**
- They respond to "podiatrist recommended" and "immediate relief".
- Hoka, OOFOS and Vionic dominate here.
- OOFOS buyers credit the rocker plus wide toe box: "Wide toe box and rocker bottom make them extra helpful".

**Neuropathy, diabetes and swelling**
- They want: adjustable fit for swelling, a large opening, and nothing pressing.
- Wins: "I have neuropathy and these shoes help with the pain... I don't have to bend over" (Skechers Flex); "numbness in my feet and these shoes really feel good" (Scurtain).
- Failures: "Velcro doesn't stay closed on my swollen feet" (Scurtain); "Not good support for diabetic feet. Terribly painful for heels" (ITAZERO).

**Bunions and hammertoes (wide feet)**
- The toe box has to deliver what the listing says.
- Coral's bunion claim backfired in reviews.
- Wins: Skechers Flex, Go Walk Joy ("senior with bunions and hammertoes"), ITAZERO ("hammer toe and nodules"), STUNAHOME ("doesn't hurt my bunion").
- "Lately my feet have gotten wider" (ITAZERO) is an age-related trigger.

**Plus-size and larger feet (limited evidence)**
- I read no comments about body size or weight.
- Large-size and wide-width mentions: "I'm 67 with a sz 12" (Skechers Flex), "women's size 11.5 feet" (Kita), "size 11-11.5 and these are true to size" (ITAZERO), "clunky... Not a good look for a larger sized foot" (Kita).
- This needs another source, such as Reddit, our own reviews or surveys.
