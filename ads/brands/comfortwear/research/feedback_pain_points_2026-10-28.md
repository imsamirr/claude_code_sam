# Feedback sheet: pain points, enemies and customer language (mined 2026-10-28)

Source: "ComfortWear Customer Feedback (Responses)" Google Sheet, **18,452 responses, Apr 2023 – Oct 27 2026**
(2023: 1,748 · 2024: 4,406 · 2025: 8,892 · 2026: 3,406). Comfort scores: 1★ 8,421 · 2★ 3,162 · 3★ 2,813 ·
4★ 1,434 · 5★ 2,622, so about 78% are unhappy raters and 22% happy ones (correction to §9 of analysis.md,
which said only 1–3★ customers answer). The raw sheet has emails and order numbers: only aggregates and short
anonymous quotes are kept here. Re-mine with the Drive connector (download as text/csv) when the routine
needs fresher numbers.

## 2. What they tried before (the "enemy" list for copy)

Current solution answers (n = 12,223): insoles/orthotics/inserts **13.0%** (by far #1), pain pills
(ibuprofen, Tylenol, Aleve) 3.4%, stretching/exercises/rolling a ball 3.3%, massage 2.7%, ice/heat/Epsom
soaks 2.7%, sneakers/running shoes 2.4%, Hoka 2.3%, creams/Voltaren/CBD 1.6%, podiatrist 1.4%, compression
socks 1.3%, Brooks 1.0%, night splints/braces 1.0%, Skechers 0.8%, barefoot shoes 0.8%, clogs/Crocs 0.7%,
cortisone shots 0.4%, surgery 0.3%.
→ Ladder/"tried everything" ads should name: **insoles, ibuprofen, stretching, ice, Hokas/running shoes,
compression socks, cortisone** in that order of how common they are.

## 3. Why they bought / what they look for

- #1 reason (n = 17,960): comfort 39%, work/shifts/standing 16.5%, foot pain 14.4%, saw an ad/reviews/
  recommended 12.2%, looks/cute 8.3%, walking 7.2%, plantar fasciitis 4.3%, arch support 3.2%,
  wide/toe box 2.8%, price 2.1%, **travel/vacation 1.6% (291 answers)**, slip-on/no laces 1.5%.
- Looking for (n = 15,666): comfort 68.7%, support/arch 20.9%, fit/size 7.9%, style 6.4%, wide/roomy 5.3%,
  durability 4.7%, cushion 3.9%, slip-on 2.2%, non-slip 1.1%.
- Other products wanted: socks/compression, insoles, wide widths and half sizes, dress/work shoes, sandals.

## 4. Customer language for hooks (short, anonymous, lightly trimmed)

- Shifts: "Comfortable shoes to sustain a 12hr shift" · "No back or feet pain after a 13hr shift" ·
  "On my feet all day on concrete floors" · "Pain in feet and knees after standing for 12 hours" ·
  "After 10 hour days I can barely walk" · "She's on her feet 10 hours, longer if she works a double"
- Pain: "Throbbing, burning, aching" · "My feet hurt so bad at the end of the day I can barely walk" ·
  "Burning pain in the palm of my foot" · "Sore foot in the morning or after sitting a long time" ·
  "My heels hurt to the point I feel like I can't walk on them"
- Swelling: "My feet swell and hurt from being on them all day" · "I stand 10 hours a day… pain and swelling
  in my feet and ankles"
- Travel: "To walk in Europe on uneven terrain" · "Planning to do a lot of walking for a trip coming up" ·
  "A comfortable shoe for walking on vacation"
- Bunions/wide: "Room for my bunions without discomfort" · "Wide toe box and good arch support"
- Switch/price: "Buy expensive shoes every 3 months" · "Expensive inserts and New Balance shoes" ·
  "My old brand only lasted a few months before killing my feet and back" · "Very expensive shoes and
  expensive insoles"
- Look: "A comfortable, pretty shoe I can wear for a long time" · "My foot problem, but also that they are cute"

## 5. Warnings to design around

- **Sizing** is the #1 product issue: good fit only 39.8%; too small 33%, too big 27%. Don't promise fit;
  a size-guide concept is worth testing.
- **Don't overclaim the toe box**: several unhappy answers say "you said wide toe box… wrong". Show room for
  bunions; avoid "extra wide".
- **Rocker sole surprises people** ("heel too high for the toe, foot falls forward after nine hours"): set
  expectations (what-to-expect format), don't hide it.
- **Guarantee**: complaints quote a "45-day money back… a lie": always 30 days, exact approved wording.
- **12-hour claims backfire** when pain comes back at hour 5: say "built for 12-hour shifts", not "no pain
  for 12 hours".
