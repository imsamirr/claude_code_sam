## 2026-10-30 (first deep pull: 259 ads; Hike 98 / 75 active, Stepprs 161 / 144 active)

### Hike Footwear (barefoot / zero-drop shoes, 45-75 audience)
- **Hooks that run longest:** "You should not have to choose between looking elegant and feeling safe" (79d video); "Your feet weren't designed for stiff, narrow shoes" (78d); "Built to carry extra weight comfortably" (49d, plus-size); "Best Sellers. Almost Sold Out." (44d).
- **New this month (aggressive, fear-based):** fall prevention for 65+: "Every year, over 14 million Americans over 65 fall…", "No More Fear of Falling", "Prevent Falls With Better Balance". Plus-size: "If you're a heavier woman, you've probably noticed something most brands won't admit. Your shoes were made for someone else's body."
- **Structure:** enemy (narrow toe box, raised heel, stiff/thick soles) → mechanism (nerve endings, ground feel, alignment) → ✅ feature list → social proof number ("2M / 2.4M / 4M+ happy walkers": inconsistent) → risk reversal ("Try them risk-free", "Feel the difference or you don't pay").
- **Offers:** "Biggest sale of the year", "Up to 50% / 40% off", scarcity "Only 200 pairs left", "Ends midnight", "Gift for Mom".
- **Proof devices:** named testimonial with age ("– Maria, 58"), "Doctor recommended", "Google rated 4.x", "Best Walking Shoe 2026!" titles.
- **Claims we must NOT copy:** prevents falls, relieves knee pain and swelling, restores balance, "no more foot, knee or back pain", grounding/electrical-charge claims.
- **Our counter:** they sell thin "barefoot" soles; their own copy says "thick soles block balance signals". Our angle: cushioned rocker sole + arch support for people who stand 12 hours or walk 20,000 steps (barefoot shoes are a hard switch for sore heels). Their plus-size angle overlaps ours: answer with "wide, stable base + cushion that doesn't flatten".

### Stepprs (massage insoles; plantar fasciitis, 10-12 hour shift workers)
- **Evergreen winners (400+ days):** testimonial-first primary text: "I've tried so many insoles for my plantar fasciitis — nothing ever really helped… until I found Stepprs. Within a few days, the stabbing heel pain… I wear them in my work boots for 10-hour shifts, and for the first time in years, I don't come home with aching feet." Title: **"Walk on Clouds — Or It's FREE."**
- **Structure:** customer quote (job + hours + pain) → ✅ outcome bullets ("Finish a 12hr shift with zero pain", "Fits any work boot", "Instant relief") → stacked offer ("Free Gift. 60% off. Fast shipping. 90-day guarantee.") → challenge close ("You've seen the ads. You know what these do. The only question is whether you want to keep waking up with that same foot pain…").
- **Other running angles:** anti-counterfeit ("❌ Fake Sites = Fake Comfort ❌ … Amazon, eBay, Temu"); "Insoles that go flat in weeks" enemy; mechanism names ("Targeted Pressure Mapping", "Dead Foam"); neuropathy (diabetic testimonial, "activate healthy circulation"); holiday-tagged 60% off ("Halloween / Labor Day deal").
- **Proof devices:** "Dr. Thomas" quote, "100,000+ happy customers", "podiatrist-recommended, clinically tested, last 12+ months", "Verified Customer" names.
- **Claims we must NOT copy:** zero pain, instant relief, years of pain gone, podiatrist-recommended, clinically tested, circulation, 90-day guarantee (ours is 30), fast shipping.
- **Our counter:** insoles still go inside the shoe that pinches: we are the whole shoe (wide toe box + arch + cushion + grip). Their best line structure ("job + hours + what she tried + first time in years") maps exactly onto our Emily RN winner.

### Patterns to use in our batches (with OUR claims only)
1. Title = promise + risk reversal: "Walk All Shift. Love Them or Your Money Back." (30-day).
2. Testimonial-first primary text with job, hours, what she tried, "first time in years" (Emily RN, Trustpilot).
3. Named enemy: running shoes / insoles that go flat / narrow toe boxes, then our mechanism.
4. Challenge close: "You've seen the ads. The only question is whether you want to keep limping to your car after every 12."
5. Specific numbers: hours, steps, days on trips, pairs bought; age in testimonials ("– Linda, 61") only from real reviews.
6. Stacked offer line, ours exactly: "40% OFF + FREE SHIPPING + 30-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE".
