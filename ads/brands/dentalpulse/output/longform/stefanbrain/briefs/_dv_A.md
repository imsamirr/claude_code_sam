# Brief: DentalPULSE scrolling long-form videos DV13, DV14, DV15 (angle: hospital pneumonia, the dentures nobody cleaned in the hospital)

Please write three scrolling-video scripts for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The format limits and the return fields are at the bottom; the facts are below.

## The angle and why
Hospital pneumonia. When an older person is in the hospital or rehab (surgery, a fall, a procedure), their dentures sit in a plastic cup on the tray table, rinsed at best, while they lie flat, sedated and on pain meds, with weak swallow and cough reflexes. O'Donnell 2016 found pneumonia germs on the dentures of about 64% of elderly hospital patients sampled; Quinn 2014 showed basic daily oral care cut non-ventilator hospital pneumonia by about a third; Iinuma 2015 and aspiration while sedated/asleep. Last week's videos told aspiration pneumonia at home (sleeping in dentures) and in a nursing home; this week the setting is a hospital or rehab stay, and the enemy is the denture that went in dirty and stayed dirty. Competitor proof that pneumonia long-form runs: Ploise's ER story "bacteria from her dentures had gotten into her lungs while she slept" ran 153 days; NovaPod's furious-wife hospital story ran 99 days. Aggressive: the narrators blame the dentures outright and say what it cost them.

## The three stories (narrator details are facts to build on; names, lines and the hook wording are yours)
- **DV13 (VOICE: this one gets the voice-over).** Persona: denture caregiver (spouse). A man, about 60, from Baltimore, Maryland, a city bus mechanic. His wife, about 61, had a planned hip replacement in early spring; her dentures sat in the hospital cup on the tray table for five days and nobody brushed or soaked them; on day four she got hospital pneumonia and her "routine" stay became weeks. Planned hook type: a neglected object and a count of days (the cup nobody touched). Messenger idea from his world: a respiratory therapist or the nurse on the rehab floor.
- **DV14 (music only).** Persona: denture caregiver (grandchild). A woman, about 34, from Richmond, Virginia, a hospital patient-transport aide who wheels 30+ beds a shift and sees the denture cups on every tray. Her own grandfather, about 80, goes in for a gallbladder procedure in late summer. Planned hook type: an insider's pattern she can't unsee (what the cups on the tray tables tell her). She is not a nurse or doctor.
- **DV15 (music only).** Persona: denture wearer, 60+, a man. About 72, a retired pipe welder from Birmingham, Alabama, the patient himself: knee replacement in winter, the surgery went perfectly, pneumonia on day three. Planned hook type: contradiction (the surgery was perfect; the thing in the cup nearly killed him). Messenger: someone from his world (a fellow patient in rehab, a granddaughter in nursing school, a hospital chaplain).
