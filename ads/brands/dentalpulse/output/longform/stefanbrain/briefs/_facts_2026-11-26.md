## DentalPULSE's ONE hero mechanism (every angle traces back to it)
Bacteria and yeast live in a biofilm inside the tiny scratches and pores of dentures, night guards, retainers and aligners. Brushing, soaking and tablets only clean the surface (and brushing adds scratches), so the film grows back, the appliance yellows, smells, and goes back in the mouth loaded. 42,000 Hz ultrasonic cleaning (42,000 tiny implosions a second) plus medical-grade UV-C in one 5-minute cycle reaches where brushes and tablets can't.
