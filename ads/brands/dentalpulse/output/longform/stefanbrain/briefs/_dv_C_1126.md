# Brief: DentalPULSE scrolling long-form videos DV31, DV32, DV33 (angle: the night guard and the chest infection every winter)

Please write three scrolling-video scripts for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The format limits and the return fields are at the bottom; the facts are below.

## The angle and why
Respiratory infections from a dirty night guard (the owner's own angle idea: "respiratory issues from night guards"). The guard sits wet in a closed case about 16 hours a day, then goes in the mouth all night where you breathe and swallow over it; the film in its pores keeps seeding the throat and chest; a rinse or brush doesn't touch it. It's winter now: the season of "the cough that won't leave". Core emotion: betrayal and exhaustion ("I did everything right and kept getting sick"). Aggressive: the narrators blame the guard outright for the bronchitis/pneumonia and what it cost them (missed work, antibiotics, a season lost). Last week's mold-in-the-case videos were about mold you can see; this angle is the infection you can't explain until someone connects the dates.
