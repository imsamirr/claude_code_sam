# Brief: DentalPULSE long-form statics DLF19 and DLF20

Please write two long-form static ads (one phone photo + a long first-person story as the Meta primary text) for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). Both are net-new. The concept facts are below; the product, offer, claims, research and our winners' DNA follow; the format limits and return fields are at the bottom.

## DLF19: teen retainer, an open letter to parents whose kids get braces off this Christmas
- **Persona:** parent of a teen with retainers.
- **Narrator:** a man, about 45, a firefighter in Providence, Rhode Island, father of a 16-year-old daughter whose braces (about $6,800) came off last Christmas. Within the year: a cloudy, smelly retainer kept in a closed case in her backpack, tablets that made it brittle, a cracked retainer ($400+ replacement), teeth starting to shift while she waited, and white spots / a cavity at her checkup. He writes it as an open letter to other parents in December.
- **Planned hook type:** open letter / warning to a named group of readers (new for us; timely for December braces-off season). **Open loop:** "the expensive part isn't the braces", what it actually was.
- **Lead type:** problem-solution lead; awareness: problem-aware. Messenger: someone from his world (a paramedic on his crew whose wife is an orthodontic assistant). A natural P.S.: the Pod as a Christmas gift for the day the braces come off; the free travel bag for her backpack.
- **Aggressive:** white spots/cavity and sore throats blamed on the dirty retainer. No whitening promise.

## DLF20: clear aligners (partner's) + smell, mold and cavities, an organic "AITA" post
- **Persona:** clear aligner wearer, 25–45 (told by the partner who lives with it).
- **Narrator:** a woman, about 30, a pharmacy tech in Jersey City, New Jersey. She threw her fiancé's aligner trays in the trash (about $3,000 in) because of the smell and a black-green film in the case, and posts "AITA?" style on a forum. The story unfolds as the post plus updates: the fight, his dentist finding early decay under the trays, his sore throats, what they tried (soaking, brushing, tablets, a $30 pod), and the fix.
- **Format:** an organic forum post with the brand held back to a late "EDIT:" / "UPDATE:" since people asked what they bought (Orthora's criteria → late EDIT blog format; market study 2026-11-16). For this concept the brand can sit at about 80–90% instead of 50–65%; give the criteria list before it.
- **Planned hook type:** AITA confession (new for us). **Open loop:** the verdict and whether the wedding is still on.
- **Lead type:** story lead; awareness: unaware. Still close with the evergreen offer, guarantee and P.S. lines after the EDIT.
- **Aggressive:** the cavity and throat infections blamed on the trays.
