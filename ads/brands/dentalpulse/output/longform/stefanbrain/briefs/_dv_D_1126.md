# Brief: DentalPULSE scrolling long-form videos DV34, DV35, DV36 (angle: adhesive and the burning palate: denture stomatitis)

Please write three scrolling-video scripts for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The format limits and the return fields are at the bottom; the facts are below.

## The angle and why
Denture stomatitis fed by adhesive. Every day a layer of adhesive paste goes under the plate; it never fully comes off, and with the film in the acrylic's pores it makes a warm, damp, sealed layer where Candida yeast thrives. The palate turns red and burns; antifungal rinses work for a week, then the yeast re-seeds from the plate (up to about two-thirds of denture wearers get stomatitis; overnight wear and poor cleaning raise the risk). Brushing and tablets don't get the paste residue out of the pores. Feedback/winners: "adhesive/Fixodent goo", "jelly in the morning". Core emotion: shame and the daily pain ("I thought it was just what dentures feel like"). Aggressive: the narrators blame the plate and the paste layer outright. Polident and Fixodent may be named as the old method only (no brand attacks beyond that).

## The three stories (narrator details are facts to build on; names, lines and hook wording are yours)
- **DV34 (music only).** Persona: denture wearer, 60+, a woman. About 70, a retired bowling-alley owner in Wilmington, Delaware; three tubes of adhesive a month, a burning roof of her mouth for two years, she stopped eating oranges and tomatoes. Hook type: a counted ritual (tubes of paste). Reveal setup: the denturist appointment (she apologizes for the mess on the plate).
- **DV35 (music only).** Persona: denture wearer, 60+, a man. About 68, a retired auctioneer in Springfield, Illinois, whose whole career was his mouth; at a charity auction his plate slipped and the burn made him stop mid-call. Hook type: a public moment frozen mid-sentence. Reveal setup: in person, an old friend who's a retired dentist at the coffee shop.
- **DV36 (music only).** Persona: denture caregiver (son). A man, about 45, a locksmith in Reno, Nevada, caring for his father (about 74) whose palate is so raw he's living on milkshakes; the son finds the adhesive caked in the plate's grooves "like grout". Hook type: a tradesman's eye (he opens locks; he sees the gunk in the grooves). Reveal setup: self-discovery plus his dad's denturist.
