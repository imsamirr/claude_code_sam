# Brief: DentalPULSE scrolling long-form videos DV28, DV29, DV30 (angle: the tray is a lid: cavities and white spots under aligners and retainers)

Please write three scrolling-video scripts for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The format limits and the return fields are at the bottom; the facts are below.

## The angle and why
Cavities and chalky white spots under clear aligners and retainers. The tray is a lid: whatever film lives in its tiny scratches and pores is held against the enamel up to 22 hours a day; rinsing, brushing the tray (which scratches it more) and tablets clean only the surface. Reddit's r/Invisalign and r/orthodontics are full of "first cavities of my life during Invisalign". Our DV11 told it as a wedding story; this week three new situations. Core emotion: betrayal ("I paid thousands to fix my teeth and they made them worse"). Aggressive: the narrators blame the trays outright and say what the fillings cost.

## The three stories (narrator details are facts to build on; names, lines and hook wording are yours)
- **DV28 (music only).** Persona: clear aligner wearer, 25–45. A woman, about 26, a nail tech in Orlando, Florida, whose clients stare at her mouth from 18 inches away all day; at month 8 her dentist finds two cavities and white spots exactly along the tray edge. Hook type: an insider's close-up angle (what her clients see). Reveal setup: the dentist appointment (she apologizes; the dentist holds the tray up to the light).
- **DV29 (music only).** Persona: parent of a teen with retainers. A man, about 50, an electrician in Rochester, New York; his son, 14, got four cavities in the year after braces came off; the retainer lived in a hoodie pocket and got rinsed under the tap. Hook type: a bill / number hook ("four fillings in a kid who…"). Reveal setup: a friend who happens to be an expert (his old high-school buddy, now an orthodontist).
- **DV30 (music only).** Persona: retainer wearer, adult. A woman, about 39, a Pilates instructor in San Diego, who has worn the same retainer every night since she was 15 and had zero cavities for 24 years, until three at once. Hook type: a broken streak. Reveal setup: self-discovery (she puts the retainer under a client's jeweler's loupe).
