# Brief: DentalPULSE scrolling long-form videos DV19, DV20, DV21 (angle: mold in a wet appliance: night guards and retainers)

Please write three scrolling-video scripts for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The format limits and the return fields are at the bottom; the facts are below.

## The angle and why
Mold. A night guard or retainer goes from a warm mouth into a closed, wet case for about 16 hours a day: a dark, damp box, the exact place mold and yeast grow. Candida is a yeast (a fungus) and lives in the plastic; black specks show up around case hinges; people report musty taste, morning sore throats, sinus trouble and coughs. Reddit r/bruxism: "it can be a friendly environment for bacterial and mold growth", "Nightguard constantly smells". Night guards are 22% of our buyers and the least happy appliance group until they switch ("gross yellow and cloudy", "never felt clean"); retainers 13% ("clear again, not milky", "tablets made my retainer brittle"). Cross-niche proof: the CPAP "mold in the air" long-form ran 109 days. Last week's DV10 was a black-dots-in-the-case story; these three must be clearly different stories and hooks. Aggressive: the narrators blame the appliance outright for their health trouble.
