# Brief: DentalPULSE long-form statics DLF17 and DLF18

Please write two long-form static ads (one phone photo + a long first-person story as the Meta primary text) for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The concept facts are below; the product, offer, claims, research and our winners' DNA follow; the format limits and return fields are at the bottom.

## DLF17: night guard switcher, "6 of 7 pods leave live bacteria" (closest to our #1 winner)
- **Persona:** switcher (burned buyer who already tried cheap pods), night guard.
- **Narrator:** a man, about 41, a high school football coach and PE teacher in Boise, Idaho, grinds hard, $650 custom guard. He has left one-star reviews for two cheap pods that died or did nothing (one from Amazon at $30, one from a Facebook ad); the guard still smells, and he gets strep-like sore throats. He was writing the third one-star review in advance. Football season into winter.
- **Format:** this concept should carry our account winner's format: the test. Let the story include his own side-by-side test (pod vs pod, day by day, his white-paper-towel or smell check), echoing the 90-day lab test of 7 leading ultrasonic cleaners in which 6 of 7 left live bacteria and DentalPULSE was #1. Keep the winners' failure modes for cheap pods ("clean for a week, then the ultrasonic weakens… no UV light").
- **Planned hook type:** the one-star reviewer / scam reversal. **Open loop:** whether the third review got posted.
- **Lead type:** proclamation/offer-adjacent lead (most aware of the batch: solution-aware switcher). Honest limits: not silent, doesn't whiten old stains.
- **Aggressive:** the live bacteria on his guard blamed for the throat infections.

## DLF18: clear aligners + white spots/cavities, the classroom petri dish
- **Persona:** clear aligner/Invisalign wearer, 25–45.
- **Narrator:** a woman, about 31, a high school biology teacher in Tulsa, Oklahoma, mid-treatment ($5,000+ aligners), rinses and brushes her trays, uses cleaning crystals/tablets. For a lab unit, her 10th graders swab objects and grow them on agar plates; a student swabs her "clean" aligner tray and it grows the busiest plate in the room. Then a white spot / early cavity at her orthodontic check-in. Fall semester.
- **Mechanism:** the tray is a lid: whatever film is on it is held on the enamel 22 hours a day; brushing scratches the tray, giving the film more places to live. Proof by measurement (market study: measurement and logs work as proof): the plates before and after the Pod (a later swab grows next to nothing). Never claim a lab result for the product beyond the approved claims; the class experiment is her story.
- **Planned hook type:** the classroom experiment / found result (new for us). **Open loop:** whose plate grew the most, and what the class saw at the end.
- **Lead type:** story lead; awareness: unaware. Messenger: a student's parent who is a dental hygienist, or the school's science department head.
- **Aggressive:** the white spot / cavity blamed outright on the dirty tray.
