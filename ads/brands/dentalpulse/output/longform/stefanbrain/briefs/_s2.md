## DLF13: dentures + the adhesive/biofilm layer, an overheard insult
- **Persona:** denture wearer, 60+, a woman who uses adhesive (Fixodent-type paste) every day plus Polident tablets.
- **Narrator:** a woman, about 69, a retired church organist / piano teacher in Albuquerque, New Mexico. At a big family event (her grandson's wedding reception or her own retirement party) she overhears two relatives behind her talking about the smell when she talks. Old adhesive layered under the plate plus the film in the acrylic: food for bacteria and yeast; her palate is red and sore (stomatitis), and her breath is the part people noticed. Spring.
- **Planned hook type:** overheard insult from behind (new for us; Forge's overheard-insult video and the revenge/vindication engine were the hottest in last week's market study, with the vindication scene taking 15–20% of the body). **Open loop:** who said it, and what she did at the next family event.
- **Lead type:** story lead with a vindication payoff; awareness: unaware → problem-aware. Messenger: someone from her world (a choir member who is a denture lab tech, her piano student's mom who is a hygienist).
- **Aggressive:** mold-like black film / yeast in the plate, the sore palate, the smell.

## DLF14: dentures + "what dental labs have used for decades", the heirloom
- **Persona:** denture wearer, 60+, cleaning with tablets and a brush (or the caregiver spouse).

Going through his late grandfather's things he finds the grandfather's dental-lab notebook / tools: the grandfather ran a small dental lab for decades, and labs and dental offices have long cleaned appliances in ultrasonic baths, never with fizzing tablets. Meanwhile his wife's (or his own) dentures have a film tablets never touch, and she's had two chest infections / a pneumonia scare (Iinuma 2015, aspiration). Fall.
- **Planned hook type:** historical secret + heirloom (new for us; YouthRite's "what US Army nurses wore in 1944" was rank 1 of 204). Only state history you know to be true (ultrasonic cleaners have been used in dental labs and offices for decades); no invented dates or quotes from real people. **Open loop:** what was written in the notebook.
- **Lead type:** secret lead; awareness: solution-aware (knows of pods, distrusts them). The criteria list comes from the grandfather's notebook; the $30 pod fails it (no UV-C, weak power).
- **Aggressive:** the chest infections are blamed on the film in the plate.
