# Brief: DentalPULSE scrolling long-form videos DV25, DV26, DV27 (angle: home for the holidays, and the dentures nobody checked: aspiration pneumonia)

Please write three scrolling-video scripts for DentalPULSE (project "Dentalpulsepro Launch"). The format limits and the return fields are at the bottom; the facts are below.

## The angle and why
Aspiration pneumonia, discovered by family home for the holidays (it's late November now; these run through December). Adult children and grandchildren see a parent's bathroom for the first time in months: the denture in a coffee mug, the soak that's been sitting for a week, the parent who now sleeps in them. In sleep the swallow and cough reflexes weaken and the germs in the denture's biofilm slip into the lungs (Iinuma 2015: sleeping in dentures more than doubled pneumonia risk in people 85+; Yoneyama 1999: daily oral care cut nursing-home pneumonia from about 19% to 11%). Mechanism: the film lives in the pores and scratches; the soak and the tablet only clean the surface. Competitor proof the pneumonia story runs: Ploise's sister-ER story ran 153 days. Core emotion for all three: guilt and dread ("I was right there and didn't look"); keep it, change narrator, situation, location and details. Aggressive: the narrators blame the dentures outright.

## The three stories (narrator details are facts to build on; names, lines and hook wording are yours)
- **DV25 (VOICE: this one gets the voice-over, write it for the ear).** Persona: denture caregiver (daughter). A woman, about 45, a freight dispatcher from Toledo, Ohio, flies home for Christmas to her father, about 83, a retired tool-and-die maker in Erie, Pennsylvania, widowed last spring. She finds his dentures in a chipped coffee mug by the sink in cloudy water nobody's changed in days, and hears him cough at night through the wall. In January he's admitted with aspiration pneumonia. Hook type: dated domestic object over the holidays (the mug). Reveal setup: a phone call with her cousin who is a respiratory therapist.
- **DV26 (music only).** Persona: denture caregiver (grandson). A man, about 38, a commercial roofer from Tucson, Arizona, at Thanksgiving at his grandmother's (about 88) house in Yuma. She hasn't taken her dentures out at night in decades ("nobody sees me without my teeth"). He's the one who carries her to the car in February. Hook type: a single-sentence open-ended moment at the table. Reveal setup: the ER doctor, then his wife who's a dental hygienist.
- **DV27 (music only).** Persona: denture caregiver (daughter-in-law). A woman, about 57, a retired Navy cook in Norfolk, Virginia. Her mother-in-law, about 86, stays in the guest room for the holidays; at 3 a.m. she hears a wet cough through the wall every night, and finds the dentures still in in the morning. Hook type: the sound through the wall. Reveal setup: in person, her neighbor who's a hospice nurse.
