### Claims guardrails for this batch
- No disease or prevention claims (pneumonia, cavities, thrush, sore throat). Health facts appear only as the narrator's experience or attributed research, never as a product outcome.
- No whitening or "like new" promise. Old yellow stains don't fully reverse; this is the #1 complaint (about 15 rows, mostly night guards).
- No "silent". Don't call tablets dangerous or poison; persulfate is "some people react", dated FDA 2008.
- No named competitor failures in long-form; use "the $30 one from Amazon".
- UV-C appears in our top winner transcripts but is not in `approved_claims`; leave it out until confirmed.
