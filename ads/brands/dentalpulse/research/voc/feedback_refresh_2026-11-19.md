## 2. What changed in the last 38 responses (12 Oct to 16 Nov)

| | Last 38 | Earlier 167 |
|---|---|---|
| Avg ★ | **3.11** | 3.57 |
| Night guard share | **50%** (19) | 20% |
| Retainer share | 21% (8) | 13% |
| Denture share | **13%** (5) | 57% |
| Bought for yellow stains | **55%** | 37% |
| Bought for bacteria | 53% | 68% |
| Bought for smell | 26% | 14% |

- **The buyer mix moved from denture wearers to night guard and retainer owners**, and their main reason to buy moved from bacteria to yellow stains.
- **Satisfaction fell with it.** In the last 38, night guard owners average 2.79★ (53% gave 1-2★) and retainer owners 2.75★ (50% gave 1-2★). The five denture buyers average 4.6★.
- **The disappointment is about set-in yellow.** Typical low-star answers: "No difference at all. Stains did not budge." "They are cleaner but haven't removed the stain." "Somewhat lighter in color." "white stuff on top of bite gaurd that won't come off." "There is no way it cleans anything like the website suggests."
- **Switchers are still the hardest to win.** 9 of the 38 had already owned an ultrasonic pod (Amazon, Zima, "Dental Pod", NovaT). Several say it is no better than one at a third of the price.
- **The promise that does land for night guards, retainers and aligners is "clearer, no film, no smell" with steady improvement:** "It's clear looking now!" "The color is clear again and the odor is gone. There was visible debris at the bottom of the bowl after the cycle completed." "Very clear aligner and bad breath is diminished." "Retainers appear clearer, still a few cloudy spots." "Slowly getting yellow stains out."
- **Denture buyers stay happy:** "No bad taste" (switched from Polident), "Didn't feel clean" before, now "Everything good."

## 3. New objections (what almost stopped them)

Price/cost dominates (about half). New this month: **"so many competing cleaning machines", "confusion with all brands", "difficult to choose"** (5 mentions). Also: "not sure it would work", "was it really needed?", "mixed reviews", "no personal testimony from friends", and no bathroom outlet (one retainer owner).

## 4. Implications for scripts (facts, not copy)

1. Keep the brand rule: for night guards, retainers and aligners, promise "stops it turning yellow / clear again / no white film / no smell". Don't promise that years of set-in yellow comes out in one cycle. Recent reviews show that promise produces 1-2★ buyers.
2. Denture wearers (60+) remain the happiest segment and match every current winning ad.
3. "So many machines, which one?" is a live objection worth answering. It fits the winning "tested 7 pods" comparison angle.
4. Visible proof ("debris at the bottom of the bowl after the cycle") is something customers notice on their own.
