User rule (2026-10-30): these are our two main rivals. Study them on every run (`atria_pull.py rivals --brand dentalpulse` → `data/core_competitors_<date>.csv`, sheets in `sheets/core_competitors_<date>_*.jpg`) and write at least 20% of each batch as direct answers or upgrades to their strongest current angles, using OUR approved claims only. Never show their logos or brand names on the image in a negative way; compare features, not unverifiable claims.

## 2026-10-30 (first read: 126 ads; Zima 85 = 50 video + 35 image, Sonic 41 = 40 image + 1 video)

### Zima Dental: authority + retainer-first + scarcity
- **Main hook is dentist authority.** "Trusted by 20,000+ Dentists", "Recommended by 7,200+ / 8,500 / 11,000+ dentists" (the count changes from ad to ad). They also say "Double Award-Winner. 5* Reviews. HSA/FSA Eligible.", "Trusted by 1M+ Happy Customers", "Stocked by 3500+ Dental Offices".
- **Dentist UGC videos** are most of their recent spend: "I'm a dentist, and here's what's in my daily routine", "The retainer cleaning hack I learned at Dental School", "As a dentist and microbiome expert, I took to the streets of London…", "brushing your retainer… creates tiny scratches where cloudy biofilm and plaque collects".
- **Copy pillars:** "Never Brush Your Retainer, Aligner or Guard Again." / "Stop cleaning your retainer with tablets, watch why." / "Soaking your aligners in a cup isn't cutting it." / "✔️ Removes plaque ✔️ Lifts yellow stains ✔️ Destroys bad smells" / "Fix your morning breath with the touch of a button".
- **Product line:** Dental Pod (multiple colors), Dental Pod Pro, and the new **Dental Pod Go** (cordless, rechargeable, "3-minute cleaning cycle", launched in the last 1–2 days with heavy video).
- **They still sell tablets:** "$60 value on us: Pro + free gift (Ultrasonic Sterilising Tablets)", "90 FREE cleaning tablets with your Dental Pod Pro". This contradicts their own "stop using tablets" line.
- **Offers:** free shipping and free returns, "Save $18" starter bundle, "limited bundle offer", "3x SOLD OUT / back in stock / limited stock", "Yes, the Dental Pod is back".
- **Statics:** pastel product patterns, a callout diagram ("Removes cloudy buildup / Kills 99.9% of bacteria / Saves time"), Trustpilot "Excellent" bar, award seals, "Clean Girl Starter Pack", a horse-sketch comparison ("Zima vs cloudy retainers").
- **Dentures barely appear.** The exceptions are one new static, "The best way to keep Dad's dentures clean.", and one FR static with a half-yellow denture.

### Sonic Dental: discount tiles
- Nearly every static is **"Limited Time Sale / Up to 30% (35%) OFF"**: the device on an open palm against a dark or purple glow, a window light, or space. Some have a press bar (Mirror, Express, Dentist, Standard) and a "4.8/5 Reviews.io" line. These have run 95–136 days.
- **Claims:** "ultrasonic + UVC", "Clinical grade", "Say goodbye to bacteria and germs", "trusted by over 500,000 customers", "1M+ sold worldwide".
- **Bundles:** Sonic Pro + Sonic Go ("2 Devices. 1 Solution.", "$60 off"), Sonic Spa v2.0 + travel bag + 12 tablets ($25 off; their longest-running ad, 561 days, a video).
- **Almost no pain, proof or customer language.** It's offer-only, aimed at retainers, aligners and night guards. No denture-specific ads.

### Where we win (use in copy, our approved claims only)
1. **Dentures:** both rivals are retainer-first. We own denture wearers, with real denture photos, the yellow → white proof and brown water.
2. **Bigger offer:** 40% OFF vs their ~30%, plus FREE case and FREE travel bag, FREE shipping, and a **120-day** money-back guarantee. Sell the Pod at $99.95 against "~$180 pods" (our own Denture Bundle compare-at is $180).
3. **Tap water only, no tablets, ever, no subscription.** Zima bundles tablets with its pods ("90 free tablets") while also saying tablets don't work.
4. **5-minute deep cycle vs "3-minute" quick cycles.** Frame it as thoroughness ("5 minutes, because biofilm takes more than a rinse"). Never say theirs doesn't work.
5. **Proof over authority:** they lean on dentist counts and awards we can't match with numbers. We answer with the lab test (6 of 7 pods leave live bacteria; ours was #1), visible proof (brown water, petri/UV) and "Dentist-recommended" plus the Clinicians' Choice seal (approved, no counts).
6. **Sonic's sale tiles carry no proof.** Our offer ads always show bacteria or yellow proof next to the offer.

### Guardrails
- No Zima or Sonic logos or names on images. "Other pods", "the $180 pod" and "pods that need tablets" are fine. Primary text may name them only if the user approves.
- Don't claim their product fails, breaks, or that their dentist counts are fake. Compare design features we can verify: tap water vs tablets, 5-minute cycle, guarantee length, what's included.
