# Christmas email research (Atria, ads launched Dec 5–20, 2025)

Atria indexes ads, not emails, so these are the holiday hooks and offer framings brands ran in paid social in that window (searched: "Christmas sale gift", holiday/festival theme in health, beauty, e-commerce and home, and "Christmas stocking gift guide").

| Pattern | Who ran it | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| "Black Friday savings EXTENDED" (launched Dec 5) | Ideal Image (many variants) | Email 1: Black Friday pricing extended for Christmas |
| "The gift that keeps on giving" | Ideal Image | Email 3 subject/hero |
| "This Holiday, give X the upgrade they deserve: 30% off + free shipping" | Odenson (ran ~354 days) | Email 2 framing: give Mom/Dad the upgrade from tablets |
| "The stocking stuffer that actually gets used" | Hally | Email 3 (night guards / retainers) |
| "Give the gift of warmth" | ORORO | "Give the gift of a clean smile" |
| Free gift + gift packaging included | Healo, TheManCompany, Ninetwofive, A Day In History | Lead with the FREE case + travel bag as "gifts inside the gift" |
| Year-end sale / final copies | TheManCompany, Elvasma | Email 5: last chance (end date placeholder, no invented scarcity) |

Not used: invented scarcity ("deal almost gone", "thousands ordering every day"), discount codes, and any delivery-by-Christmas promise (brand rule: no shipping-speed promises).
