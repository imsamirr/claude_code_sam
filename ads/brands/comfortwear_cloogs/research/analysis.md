# Cloog Cloud Slippers: research (2026-10-31)

Product line under Comfort Wear (`ads/brands/comfortwear_cloogs/`). Separate folder from `comfortwear/`
because the product rules, references and personas differ (unisex slipper vs women's work shoe).

## 1. Product (comfortorthowear.com/products/cloogs-cloud-slippers)

- Unisex corduroy-plush slipper clog, sherpa fleece lining, thick cushioned off-white sole.
- 6 colors: blue, green, pink, grey, taupe/brown, orange. Sizes W 4.5–13 / M 3.5–12 (size up one).
- $89.95, no compare-at price. The site currently runs "HALLOWEEN SALE: UP TO 40% OFF".
- Page claims: cloud-like comfort, extra-fleece cushion, non-slip grip, indoor & outdoor (not in rain),
  machine washable (cold, air dry). Also on the page, but not used in ads until confirmed: 30-day money-back,
  free returns, "Trusted by 500,000+", "Rated 4.87/5", foot-pain help (bunions/PF).
- Review language to borrow: "like a little hug for my feet", "pop outside without changing shoes",
  "bought for friends and family, then treated myself", "used to replace my slippers every year or two",
  "first thing in the morning".

## 2. Competitors (Atria, `research/data/competitor_ads.csv`, sheets in `research/sheets/`)

**Pluffi** sells a near-identical corduroy slipper (99 ads: 50 image, 49 video). It's the closest
competitor.
- Longest-running ads are video UGC testimonials: "I never thought I'd be this excited about slippers"
  (721d), "Slip into Cloud Nine … loved by 71,500 happy feet" (423d), "The ultimate cozy gift for anyone
  who's always cold. Buy 2, Get 1 FREE" (385d), "Made for tired, achy feet" (380d), "thick cushioned sole
  … walking on a soft cloud" (371d).
- Statics: "Black Friday Secret Deal: Buy one get one" red sunburst; "Mix & match bundle: up to 52% off
  + free gifts"; "The slipper guests ask about" (hand-held + color lineup + 3 icons + green offer bar);
  "Sorry, Pluffi fans: our sale is ending today"; "Limited edition: only 50 pairs per size"; bed flat-lay
  with handwritten sticky notes ("Cheapest they will EVER be!!", "52% off", "Free gifts", cash);
  "\"The best gift idea for people with cold feet\"" arrow callouts; Halloween black grid.
- Offer stack: 52% off / $25 off + free gifts + free shipping; BOGO; B2G1.

**HappyFlops**: EVA slides, mostly Nordic/NL/PL markets, with a winter slipper restock now. 61 brand ads
plus many creator whitelisted ads.
- Long-running statics: color-dot offer tile "Up to 40% off" (135d); simple vs HappyFlops X/check
  comparison (124d); text-card testimonial overlaid on a lifestyle photo (106d); iMessage notification
  from Mom about the "buy 3 pay 2" deal (100d); "!! DON'T CHOOSE THESE !!" sticker + arrow (84d);
  big % type-only tile (83d); review card + "Buy 2 get 1 free" pill (91d); gift box scene (79d).
- Offers: 2+1 free, buy 3 pay 2, up to 40% off, creator codes.
