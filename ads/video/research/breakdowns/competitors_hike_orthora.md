Source: `ads/video/research/data/competitor_videos.json` (video, running ≥ 30 days, ranked by impressions, variants collapsed). All 20 are **active** with a **stable** trend. Rank = impression rank inside that brand's pool (Hike pool 1,559 ads, Orthora pool 166). Text sizes are measured on frames scaled to 1080 px wide (1080×1920 for 9:16, 1080×1350 for 4:5). "Line ≈ N px" is the height of one text band (cap height plus descenders). "Box" is the white or colored caption plate.
