# Comfort Wear organic-post video playbook

*Research date: 2026-11-13. Sources: Atria library (Meta ads, run days as reported on 2026-11-13; I pulled frames from each video with ffmpeg to read the on-screen captions), web trend reports (cited in section 3). Reddit could not be reached from this environment.*

Use this to pick **10 different concepts per weekly batch**: one format per video, spread across audiences, and no format repeated from the previous two weeks. W46 already used: Disney plus-size "hour 9", Disney shoe ratings, "enfermeras" meme, nurse "regret not sooner", "best 12-hour-shift shoes".

---

## 1. Rules of the style

The approved look (set A) is a **phone video that a real woman would post to her own feed**. It has one first-person caption, no voiceover, music underneath, and 2 to 4 handheld shots that tell a tiny story: problem, then the product mentioned casually, then the payoff. The ad copy and the end card do the selling. The video itself never shows a price, discount, logo card or feature list. In the new references, the longest-running ads are even simpler. Shop Sunny Tails has run 397 days on a 6.5 s single shot with one caption. Alicia Darling has run 256 days with a 9.7 s silhouette, a mirror shot and the product. Happy Tuesdays has run 120 days with a 10 s single shot of a man dancing.

- **Length:** 6 to 15 s. Set A runs 6.8 to 15.1 s, and the long runners are 6.5 to 12 s. Anything over 20 s should be a list or day-in-the-life format.
- **Caption:** one sentence or two, first person, written the way people text: lowercase is fine, with 1 or 2 emoji (😮‍💨 😭 🙏 👀 🫠 💀 ✨ >>>). The caption either stays the whole time (most set A) or changes once per shot in "letter" and "running sentence" formats (KatKin, Dr. Woof socks).
- **Caption styling, three looks all seen in long runners:**
  1. **TikTok Classic box:** white rounded box with black text (EllaOla, Hug Sleep Pod, Basic Buzz, Auto Accident, Bee Balm 154 d). This is our default.
  2. **Plain white text with a thin shadow and no box** (Flo, KatKin, Vivanmn, Alicia Darling, Sand & Sky, Shop Sunny Tails). It reads as more casual, so use it for meme and diary formats.
  3. **Heavy white meme text with a black stroke** (Happy Tuesdays, Friday Beers, Arctic Goddess, Emmafy). Use it for reaction memes.

  Serif "Instagram Classic" text (Savannah Bell, UrbanStems, Poshan) works for cozy and gift posts.
- **Placement:** the caption goes in the upper third or a narrow column beside the subject (Hug Sleep Pod), never over the face or the shoe, and inside the 4:5 safe area.
- **Shots:** real, slightly imperfect phone footage: car selfies after a shift, hallway walking, a mirror, a hand pulling the knit, feet up on the couch. Each shot runs 1.5 to 4 s, with no transitions beyond hard cuts.
- **Product mention:** name it the way a friend would: "these stretchy Comfort Wear ones", "my purple sneakers". It appears in the caption at most once, or only in the ad copy.
- **Payoff over features:** say "hour 12 and I'm still walking normal", not "cushioned rocker sole".
- **Offer stays out of the footage and caption.** The offer lives in the end card (user rule: always append the brand's end card: Black Friday through Nov 23, regular after; Spanish card for Spanish ads) and in the primary text.

## 2. Format library (30 formats)

Each entry gives the template, real references (advertiser, Atria id, days running, length), a shot recipe and a ready Comfort Wear caption. Days running are as of 2026-11-13. All Comfort Wear lines avoid guarantees, doctor or podiatrist claims, review counts or stars, and cure or treatment language.

### F1. POV: problem, then fix (two caption boxes)
- **Template:** top box "POV: [pain in my words]. [what it cost me]." Bottom box appears on shot 2: "Then I started wearing [casual product name]. [payoff]."
- **Examples:** The Mealtime Method / EllaOla `m961645566525315` (16 d, 15 s); Bee Balm `m1344999900895830` (154 d, 11 s, single box "POV: You finally found a lip balm that is cute and extremely hydrating"); Basic Buzz `m1668570404657353` (29 d, 6.8 s).
- **Shots:** 1) tired car selfie after a shift, hand on forehead; 2) pulling the shoe on by the tab, knit stretching; 3) feet walking a hallway; 4) thumbs up or feet up, relaxed.
- **Comfort Wear (nurse):** top "POV: you work 12s and your feet start yelling by hour 6. I used to sit in my car after every shift just to take my shoes off." Bottom "Then a coworker put me onto these stretchy Comfort Wear ones. Hour 12 and I'm still walking normal 😮‍💨"

### F2. Thank you + EDIT
- **Template:** "Me [big win] without [dreaded thing] 😅 / To the [person] who told me about [product]… THANK YOU 🙏 / EDIT: since everyone keeps asking, I linked them below."
- **Examples:** Auto Accident Compensation `m1363956582376518` (26 d, 8.8 s, rising, one continuous confident walk); CAINTÈ `m1132511345896681` (19 d, "Everyone keeps asking where these are from").
- **Shots:** one 8 to 10 s continuous shot of a confident walk toward the camera: a park street, a terminal, a hospital parking lot.
- **Comfort Wear (Disney/travel):** "Me doing 28,000 steps on park day without stopping to sit every hour 😅 To the lady in the bag-check line who told me about her stretchy knit sneakers… THANK YOU 🙏 EDIT: since everyone keeps asking, I linked them below."

### F3. "when someone…" (narrow-column story)
- **Template:** "when [person] has been [suffering] for [time]… but [someone] gets them [product] and they say '[quote]'… and [payoff]…"
- **Examples:** Hug Sleep Pod `m1047219301474933` (46 d, 7 s; the caption runs as a tall narrow column left of the subject); babybub `m3103169093352991` (21 d, 12 s, "When you finally find postpartum leggings that make you feel like YOU 💖").
- **Shots:** 1) dim problem shot: worn-out sneakers by the door, or feet up and swollen-looking under a blanket; 2) the person wearing the shoes, looking to camera with a deadpan or pleased face.
- **Comfort Wear (senior/gift):** "when your mom has worn the same falling-apart sneakers for 6 years because 'nothing else fits my feet'… but you get her these stretchy ones and she says 'oh, they hug my foot'… and she walks the whole mall with you that weekend…"

### F4. Nobody told me / Nobody warns you
- **Template:** "Nobody told me [category] could be this [comfy/cute/cheap]. Ditch the [expensive habit] and [simple swap]." Meme version: "Me finding out [fact] and nobody told me 😐".

- **Shots:** one continuous demo (errands, a walking path, a classroom), or a reaction face followed by the shoe.
- **Comfort Wear (plus-size):** "Nobody told me sneakers could fit wide feet AND look cute. I stopped forcing my feet into 'normal' widths and switched to these stretchy knit ones 🫶"

### F5. Heard you were looking for…
- **Template:** "Heard you were looking for [solution] to your [specific problem]".

### F6. Letter or apology, one line per shot

## 3. Trending on TikTok now (early to mid November 2026)

**Formats and memes**
- **"I'm so sorry" reveal carousel.** 4 to 5 photos build a story, the last slide reveals the truth, and the caption says "I'm so sorry". It peaked around 408K videos, with a "perdón" version at 435K ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-trends)). This matches F6 and could also run as a photo-carousel ad.
- **"Why Would I Deceive You?"** A deadpan, over-reassuring voice clip plays over on-screen text about something that is about to disappoint. Brands use it to poke fun at their category ([New Engen](https://newengen.com/insights/tiktok-trends/)). We can't use the audio in ads unless it is in the CML, but the joke works as a caption: "my old shoes at 7am: 'why would I hurt you?'"
- **"Go, Go, Go"** (frantic lip-sync for urgent moments, fits Black Friday), **Mariah "It's Time"** (cut to the holiday reveal), **"Housewife" outfit or work reveal** (Tate McRae; POV line plus confident lip-sync), **"Dame Hombro"** shoulder bounce, Kylie "Angel walk", "costume loading" freeze-frame ([New Engen](https://newengen.com/insights/tiktok-trends/), [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-trends), [Trending4](https://trending4.com/november-2026-tiktok-trends-mariah-carey-guide/)). The "Housewife work reveal" ("feature the people behind the product doing their jobs") maps onto a nurse or teacher walking into work.
- **Tiny-text reveal** (viewers pause to read), **"Reasons why you don't need to…"** (fake-unconvincing reasons, good pre-Black Friday), **"Money can't buy happiness, but…"** montage, cozy festive countdowns, and "summer memories vs November" nostalgia ([Pepper Agency](https://www.pepperagency.com/blog/tiktok-instagram-trends-for-november-2026-and-how-brands-can-actually-use-them)).
- **Expectation vs reality check**, **no-talking micro-vlog** ("for anyone who hates talking to the camera"), and **"my swag never left"** before/after ([howtogoviral, Nov 2026 US](https://www.howtogoviral.app/tiktok-trends)).
- **Volume outlier data, measured 2026-11-13** ([tryvolume.app/working](https://tryvolume.app/working)):
  - Cross-niche: "finished result flashed in second one" got 20x (family/pets) and 17x (fitness before/after). "Long text over a 6-s clip" got 27x. "Silent red X then green check demo" got 12x.

## 4. Music guide

**Paid ads must use licensed audio.** On TikTok, business and ad accounts are limited to the **Commercial Music Library**, and trending catalog sounds aren't cleared for commercial use, even if a creator account could use them ([Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-licensing-rules), [Foxi Music](https://www.foximusic.com/blog/commercial-music-licensing-tiktok-guide/), [Planoly](https://api.planoly.com/blog/commercially-licensed-vs-trending-sounds-what-can-brands-use)). On Meta, business accounts lose the licensed Reels library and fall back to **Meta Sound Collection** ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/ipad-app/402084904469945)). Reels that use copyrighted music can't be boosted, and Reels ads should use original or royalty-free audio ([Soundstripe on IG ads](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/royalty-free-music-for-instagram-ads), [Sonilo](https://sonilo.com/ai-music/royalty-free-music-for-instagram-reels)); verify in Ads Manager. Some inspiration ads use what sound like commercial songs (Speak uses a Spanish track, Crown & Paw a Vietnamese meme song). That is not proof we can.

**Sources we can use:** TikTok CML, Meta Sound Collection, generated royalty-free tracks (Atria `ad_maker_generate_music`, which is paid, so ask first), and the brand's existing tracks in `ads/brands/comfortwear/video/music/` (`park_whimsical_100bpm.mp3`, `upbeat_pop_vlog_105bpm.mp3`). Keep music at about -18 to -14 LUFS under any ambient sound. Cut on the beat, and put the first cut on the first downbeat (about 0.5 to 1 s).

## 5. Do and don't

**Do**
- Write the caption first, out loud, as something a real woman would post. If it sounds like an ad, rewrite it.
- Open on the problem or a person, not the product. The shoe appears by shot 2.
- Keep one caption per video (or one line per shot for F6 and F30), inside the 4:5 safe area, off faces and the shoe.
- Use real-feeling footage: car, hallway, kitchen, mirror, park. Every source clip is used once per batch (`check_batch.py`).
- Show a woman wearing the shoe, every time. Men only as gift-givers or in the background.
- Mix lengths in each batch: at least two micro loops (5 to 8 s), five 9 to 15 s, and up to three list or day-in-the-life videos (20 to 30 s).
- Spread each batch across audiences: roughly 3 nurse/healthcare, 2 Disney/travel, 1 plus-size, 1 teacher, 1 senior/gift, 1 retail/restaurant, 1 seasonal gift.
- Rotate caption styling (Classic box, plain shadow text, meme stroke) so the batch doesn't look templated.
- Blur Skechers and any competitor logos on "old shoe" shots.
- Allowed payoff language: "comfy", "stretchy", "fits my wide feet", "I'm still walking normal at hour 12", "my feet aren't screaming", "cute AND comfortable".

**Don't**
- No price, discount, "60% off", feature list or logo card in the video body. (The end-card rule in `brand.json` needs your call; see section 1.)
- No money-back guarantee, free or easy returns, or fast shipping. Several inspiration ads (Flo "full refund", Dr. Woof "99-day money-back guarantee", Hollow) do this, and we can't copy it.
- No "podiatrist/doctor recommended", "orthopedic approved", "treats/fixes/cures [plantar fasciitis, neuropathy, bunions]", "pain-free" or "relief". Nurse Babes' "treat all foot conditions" is exactly what we must not say.
- No review counts, star ratings or "9/10" scores. Use word verdicts (F18).
- No voiceover by default; music only.
- Don't use trending commercial songs in paid ads, even if a competitor appears to. Match their mood with licensed tracks.
- No Disney characters, castle close-ups or logos as the focus, and avoid putting "Disney" in on-screen text of paid ads. Prefer "park day" or "the parks", and let the ad copy say Disney if legal is OK with it.
- No jokes about patients, and no filming on hospital units or showing patient information.
- Don't reuse a format from the last two weekly batches, or two formats with the same opening shot in one batch.
