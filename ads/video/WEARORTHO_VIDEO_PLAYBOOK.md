# WearOrtho DR video playbook (master)

The house spec for briefing, editing and iterating WearOrtho (ComfortWear / OrthoStretch) video ads. Distilled from:

1. **DR editing theory**: `research/dr_editing_playbook.md` (47 sources read in full + Recharm's "10 Rules of DTC Editing" transcript).
2. **Our own winners**: every video ad in the WearOrtho Meta accounts with > $20k CAD lifetime spend. That is 52 ads and 39 unique creatives; 30 of them were analysed frame by frame, and 9 from thumbnail + copy only because Meta no longer serves those files. Detail is in `research/breakdowns/wearortho_*.md`.
3. **Competitors / best-in-class DR**: the top 10 videos by impressions, running ≥ 30 days, for each of Hike, Orthora, Stepprs, Xstance, Viasox, Everstride, Hollow Socks, OneCompress, KittySpout and BioRoot Labs (100 videos). Detail is in `research/breakdowns/competitors_*.md`.

Numbers are in `research/data/structure_stats.md`. **Where our own data disagrees with generic advice, our data wins.** These are flagged ⚠.

## 2. House edit spec (1080×1920 master)

### 2.1 Structure and timing (long-form cold traffic, 90–180 s)

| Beat | Time / % | Edit |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | **One held shot** (problem, ASMR, landmark, POV or test). Boxed two-tone headline on frame 1, spoken verbatim (or extended) as VO line 1. Optional punch line pops in at ~1.75 s. |
| Re-hook / setup | 3–10 s | First hard cut at ~3 s, then 1.5–2 s shots. A 2nd text beat around 5–6 s ("RESULTS WERE EMBARRASSING"). |
| Problem + failed fixes | 10–35 % | B&W grade or red ✕ stamps on the old shoe, the "wrong" fixes (running shoes, insoles, painkillers, $400 orthotics), red pain glow on X-ray/3D foot. |
| Product reveal + mechanism | 35–60 % | Hero reveal, then the proven mechanism module: wide toe box · real arch support · slip-resistant · stretch knit for swelling · built for standing. Glowing X-ray, 3D foam/sole, bunion toe-pull. |
| Proof | 60–80 % | Creator grid "500,000 women", relief timeline ("within days… by week two…"), washing-machine, reviews. |
| Offer + CTA | ~80–95 % | 40 % off + 45 days + free shipping. Red "ORDER NOW" pill + bobbing chevron over the last 10–15 %. |
| End card | last 4–7 s | Shared ComfortWear bundles/% end card. |
| *(optional)* Post-CTA reel | +60–120 s | 3–5 customer selfies under a fixed bottom offer band (Xstance: extends watch time into a 2nd CTA zone). |

**Mid-video reset:** one 0.03–0.4 s stutter or flash micro-cut burst at 28–48 % of runtime (6/11 winners in batch 2).

**Short cuts (sale / retargeting, 13–45 s):** the offer goes in the first 5–7 s and is restated in a persistent sticker header (Viasox, Hollow, Orthora 08, W28/W29).

### 2.2 Pacing rules

- B-roll shots **1.5–2.0 s** (cap 3 s unless it is motion or proof). Hook hold 2.5–3.5 s. ⚠ This is slower in the hook and the same in the body versus generic "cut every 1 s" advice. The older audience needs the read time (Recharm, BioRoot).
- Talking-head formats (podiatrist, nurse creator) can hold 5–10 s if a product cut-out, score caption or graphic changes every ~5–7 s (W37: 5.75 % CTR).
- **Say it, show it:** every noun in the VO gets the literal shot on that word. Change the caption on every cut.
- Tighten VO pauses > 0.3 s. Do not hold a clip to land a music beat.

### 2.3 Text spec

| Element | Spec (measured from winners) |
|---|---|
| **Running captions** (default) | TikTok "Classic": **black TikTok Sans / Proxima Nova Semibold, sentence case, on a white rounded box**, 1–2 lines, 2–6 words, glyph line **≈ 45–55 px** (box ≈ 90–100 px per line), centred at **60–80 % from top**. |
| Alt caption (sale cuts) | White Montserrat ExtraBold, active word in a blue (#0F9BF3) box, 2 lines, ≈ 55–70 px ("Kyro sale template"). |
| Alt caption (55+ offer cuts) | Yellow heavy rounded font, dark outline, 1 line, ≈ 55–65 px, 73–77 % from top (Viasox). |
| **Hook headline** | Solid box/bar, **black + red** (#E80000–#FF0000) two-tone caps, **Montserrat ExtraBold or Oswald-style condensed**, glyph 45–75 px, punch line equal to or up to 1.5× the setup line. Either top 15–30 % or low 62–82 %. Never both zones at once. |
| Emphasis | Colour only the number or offer (green "40 % OFF", green "500,000"). Red = pain/old way, green = relief/OrthoStretch. |
| Competitor names | Masked on screen (H0ka, Br0oks, Skech*rs); logos blurred. |
| Safe zone | Keep text in y = 285–1248 and x = 120–960, which works for both Reels and TikTok. Hook headlines that sit higher must still clear the top 14 %. |

### 2.4 Graphic grammar (the shared kit)

- Red translucent ✕ stamps over old habits or competitor shoes.
- Orange outlined numerals 1/2/3 or "REASON NO. X".
- Red radial pain glow / lightning on the heel.
- Green glowing X-ray skeleton inside the shoe.
- 3D foot with orange bunion glow.
- 3D layered sole / foam-bounce render.
- Gloved hand pulling a bunion toe out of a narrow shoe.
- Green-glowing-leg stretch clip.
- 9–15 face "500,000 women" creator grid.
- Washing machine.
- B&W grade for the "before".
- Green ✓ on the hero.
- Step-counter ring.
- TikTok comment / reply stickers.

Transitions are ~100 % hard cuts. The only exceptions are a white flash, a zoom-blur into the hook, and a throw-to-lens. No whips or speed ramps.

### 2.5 Audio

A continuous VO with a low music bed (≈ 15–25 % under VO; the bed is never fully silent in winners). Use SFX sparingly: ≤ 3 per 30 s, on ✕ stamps, number pops and the offer.

## 3. Hook bank (ranked by evidence)

**Proven in our account:**
1. **Ranking / "we tested 5 shoes nurses wear"**: named competitors with scores out of 10, ours wins and is the cheapest. Use a 3-panel or 2×2 stacked shoe hook with a "TOP 5 SHOES FOR NURSES" bar (W01 $800k, W02, W17/W24/W32 Spanish $180k+).
2. **"My doctor said there's no cure…"**: authority dismissed, then personal discovery (W07, W10, W18, W19).
3. **Contrarian**: "The worst advice I ever got for travelling in Europe…" (W16, 7.0 % CTR).
4. **Nostalgia**: "Remember when nurses wore white shoes?" (W22, 7.9 % CTR).
5. **Stat shock**: "87 % of nurses wear the wrong shoes" (W23).
6. **Objection list**: "3 things that didn't fix my feet: running shoes, painkillers, $400 shoes" (W03, W04, W11).
7. **First-person number confession**: "I spent $60 on these… and I regret not buying sooner" / "23 miles in Europe" (W08, W09).
8. **Reply-to-comment asking for a discount** (W14), **reverse psychology** "then don't buy these" (W15), **persona call-out** "Mujeres curvy, atención" (W21), and **"women over 65 hate this"** (W29).
9. **Podiatrist rates shoes** with a product cut-out on his chest and a score caption (W37, senior angle).

**To test next (borrowed; the key is the competitor ad that shows it):**
1. **Stacked competitor callout**: "Nurse switched to [HOKAS & BROOKS] / Her foot pain got WORSE" (orthora_09).
2. **Delayed-hero torture test, no words for 8–12 s**: egg or book drop on generic and Skechers soles; OrthoStretch in frame but tested last. Then re-voice the same footage per persona (stepprs_01/07, up to 472 days live).
3. **"Stop / Don't X → do this instead" pill**: "STOP buying soft shoes for your plantar fasciitis" (xstance_01/04).
4. **"This is what would happen to a nurse's feet after 3 weeks of 12-hour shifts"** timeline (onecompress_02, 17 duplicates).
5. **"3 red flags when buying shift shoes. If you see #3, save your money"** (onecompress_05).
6. **"This is what $X gets you"** price anchor: coffee, cafeteria lunch, drugstore insoles, then OrthoStretch per pair (viasox_06).
7. **"Side effects" reverse psychology**: "Planning to wear these on a 12-hour shift? Hear the side effects first" (everstride_07, 468 days).
8. **Holiday POV**: "POV: it's Christmas morning and you're in your scrubs in the hospital parking lot" (viasox_10). This is timely for December.
9. **"Costs 3× more than Skechers… and keeps selling out"** in a serif box (kittyspout_01). Adapt it to our price story.
10. **Pinterest-style "HOW TO: relieve heel pain" icon grid**, zoom-blurring into the "supportive shoes" icon (xstance_09, 561 days, 36 variants).
11. **Split-screen walk** with Day 1 / 7 / 14 labels (xstance_06), and **"These were my feet" photo inset** (bioroot_06).
12. **"Okay, bet" price-objection skit**, or a "Not enough?" escalating offer stack (hollow_06, kittyspout_09). Use for the BFCM window.

## 6. Iteration protocol

1. **Lock V1 before versions** (Recharm rule 8). After that, every winner gets 3–5 hooks that differ *visually* (Meta groups text-only swaps as one ad), plus persona re-skins (nurse → senior → teacher → travel → Spanish).
2. Graft "Reply to comment" stickers and stutter re-cuts onto aging winners (hike_09).
3. Put the price in the primary text, not the video, for evergreen bodies (everstride_10, 622 days).
4. Read the metrics in this order: hook rate (3 s views ÷ impressions; target 30–40 %, below ~21 % means change the hook), then hold rate (ThruPlays ÷ 3 s plays; 40–50 % is average, 60 % is strong; low means fix pacing or the 3–10 s re-hook), then CTR (low means the offer or proof is too late or weak), then CVR (a landing-page mismatch). The full metric→fix table is in `research/dr_editing_playbook.md` §10.

## 7. Compliance guardrails

- Never pair "you/your" with a condition in the hook ("Do you have plantar fasciitis?"). Use first-person or third-person ("Nurses with heel pain…").
- No cure, heal, treat or guarantee-of-results language. Skip competitor medical claims ("100 % healed", "no surgery", mortality framing).
- Comfort, support and design claims are safe, and first-person experience is safe.
- Don't imply credentials a creator doesn't have. Keep claims we can't substantiate (customer counts, "podiatrist recommended") consistent with what is approved for the static program.

## 8. Caveats

- YouTube blocks this cloud environment. Only the Recharm reference video's transcript was read; other YouTube videos were identified by title only, and their content came from written sources.
- 9 older WearOrtho winners (2023–24) are thumbnail + copy only.
- Fonts are identified by eye. Sizes are measured on frames normalised to 1080 px wide.
- Music is inferred from audio levels; tracks were not identified.
