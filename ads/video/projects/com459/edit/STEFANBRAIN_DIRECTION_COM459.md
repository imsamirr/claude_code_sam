
## 1. Project Overview & Pacing Directives

- **Ad Code**: COM459
- **Campaign**: Travel / Japan Iteration (Sister to approved COM458 NYC, scaled from COM376 Disney winner)
- **Product**: ComfortWear Ortho Stretch (Slip-on knit sneaker, wide toe box, stretch knit upper, anatomical arch insole, orange/black slip-resistant rubber outsole)
- **Execution Style**: Authentic UGC Mash-Up (High-contrast organic TikTok travel footage + competitor footwear comparisons + 3D podiatric animations + Dr. Moore clinical authority + product proof)
- **Primary Objective**: Direct the pacing, footage sourcing, and on-screen text for every single spoken line to eliminate editor guesswork and prevent rapid-fire visual fatigue.

### Strict Pacing & Editing Rules (Client Directive from $20k+ Spend Data)
1. **Target Cadence (~5 Words per Cut)**: Median shot length is ~1.8 seconds. Pacing tracks the voiceover at approximately 5 words per shot, allowing visual context to register before transitioning.
2. **Hard Floor (1.2 Seconds Minimum)**: No shot may be cut under 1.2 seconds, except for a single deliberate 3-shot burst during P3 slip/fall hazard impact.
3. **Continuous Takes Only**: Sourced UGC clips must be clean, single continuous takes. Never select a TikTok clip that already contains internal creator jump cuts.
4. **Grouped Nouns over Micro-Cuts**: When the voiceover lists consecutive environments (for example: "polished subway tiles, metal train grates, and steep stone temple steps"), do not flash three separate 0.4-second stills. Group them into cohesive, breathing shots lasting 1.4s to 2.2s.
5. **Safe Zones**: Keep all primary visual subjects, hook cards, and headlines in the 9:16 vertical safe zone (clearing TikTok's right-hand engagement icons and bottom caption bar).

## 2. On-Screen Text System Architecture

Matching the proven visual structure of COM376 (Disney) and approved sister ad COM458 (NYC), the text layout uses four clear tiers and explicitly omits floating feature badges or side banners.

### Tier 1: Hook Cards (0:00 to 0:03)
- **Placement**: Top-third center, static native TikTok text overlay box or high-contrast rounded rectangular card.
- **Visual Styling**: Heavy bold sans-serif font, white text on solid dark charcoal/black rounded pill with a subtle red border or warning emoji.
- **Variants**:
  - Hook 1: `Hokas & On Clouds are the WORST shoes for Japan ❌`
  - Hook 2: `STOP packing Hokas and On Clouds for Japan 🛑`
  - Hook 3: `The #1 footwear mistake travelers make in Japan ✈️`
- **Behavior**: Remains pinned on screen through the first 3.5 seconds across the first 2 shots, then drops off cleanly as the body begins.

### Tier 2: Section Headlines (Top Center Pinned)
- **Placement**: Upper third (below status bar, above subject eyeline). Pinned during each corresponding body section.
- **Visual Styling**: Solid high-contrast text pill with bright colored accent (yellow or red highlight).
- **Exact Lineup**:
  - **P1 (Context)**: `20,000+ steps every day in Japan 🚶‍♂️`
  - **P2 (Vulnerability 1)**: `#1 - Soft foam packs flat while standing 📉`
  - **P3 (Vulnerability 2)**: `#2 - Dangerous on slick tiles, ice & snow ❄️`
  - **P4 (Vulnerability 3)**: `#3 - Narrow toe boxes crush swelling feet 🦶`
  - **P5 (Solution Transition)**: `Built for real travel, not 40-minute runs ✅`
  - **P6 (Consequence / Lifestyle)**: `Don't let sore feet cut your dream trip short 🇯🇵`
  - **P7 (Offer)**: `Holiday Sale: Up to 40% Off + 30-Day Guarantee 🎁`

### Tier 3: Running Caption Pills (Continuous Bottom-Center)
- **Placement**: Center-bottom, sitting precisely at 68% vertical height to clear the TikTok audio track name, description box, and navigation icons.
- **Visual Styling**: Dynamic karaoke-style subtitle boxes. Black semi-translucent rounded capsule, white text, 1-3 words highlighted in vibrant yellow or cyan in sync with the audio track.
- **Rule**: Every single spoken VO word must be represented in the running caption pill.

### Tier 4: Offer Stickers & Final End Card (P7 Only)
- **Mid-P7 Offer Badges**: Animated sticker pop-ins appearing during P7:
  - Badge A: `Holiday Sale: Up to 40% Off 🎁`
  - Badge B: `Under $60 a Pair (Free Shipping) 📦`
  - Badge C: `30-Day Wear-Them-Everywhere Guarantee 🛡️`
- **End Card Slate (Final 2.5s)**: High-converting split screen:
  - Top half: Clean hero loop of ComfortWear Ortho Stretch on a Tokyo crosswalk or station platform.
  - Bottom half: Solid bold CTA card: `Holiday Sale: Up to 40% Off + 30-Day Guarantee 🎁` with an animated pulsing button: `Tap Below to Claim Travel Deal ➔`.

### Explicit Text Omissions
- **No Floating Feature Pills / Side Banners**: Do not add floating tags like "Arch Support™", "Wide Toe Box", or "Knit Comfort". COM376 and COM458 proved that floating feature tags make the video feel like a cheap infomercial, breaking UGC native immersion. Feature education is carried exclusively by the voiceover, Dr. Moore, and precise macro product footage.
