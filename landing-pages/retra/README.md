# Retra Screen-Free Camera: product landing page template

`index.html` is a self-contained product page (open it in a browser; images live in `images/`).

- **Structure:** mirrors KlikCamera's product page section by section.
- **Branding:** Retro-pia's look. Cream paper, navy ink, serif headlines with a muted second line, handwritten kickers, photos as tilted prints.
- **Copy:** every word is StefanBrain's (chat `chat_50348e588b9d4d9595a63eb210f50971`). Source files are in `copy/`:
  - `01`–`08`: first draft, by section.
  - `09-stefanbrain-corrections.md`: fact/rules fixes (Disney names removed, unconfirmed specs removed, samples labelled, offer made consistent).
  - `10-stefanbrain-layout-slots.md`: short labels for the table, reviews and photo-wall headings.
  - Where `09`/`10` and the `retra-landing-page.md` master disagree, `09`/`10` win (the master kept some stale lines).
- **Personas** (from StefanBrain): 1) Screen-Fatigued Traveler (primary, Europe), 2) Mindful Family & Theme-Park Parent, 3) Ritual Gifter & Nostalgia Seeker.

## Before this goes live

- **Placeholders (highlighted yellow on the page):** price, compare price, discount, bundle and add-on prices, rating, review count, installments, stock notice, shipping times, payment methods, company name.
- **Sample content:** the pull quote, three reviews and the six community tiles are samples. Replace them with real customer content before running traffic.
- **Images:** all of them are AI-generated mockups, not photos taken on the camera. Swap in real product photos and real sample shots from the supplier unit when you have them.
- **Disney look-alike images:** `park-castle.jpg` and `park-fireworks.jpg` show a castle that closely resembles Disney's, which is a protected trademark. They're kept small (photo wall and one review card) but should be replaced or approved before ads run.
- **Confirm with the supplier:** the optical viewfinder (the copy mentions it), the filter control type, and the seven colourways.
- **Wiring:** the buttons and forms are template-only. Connect them to the store's cart and email platform.
