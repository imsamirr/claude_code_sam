### Slot 5: FAQ (6 Questions, 1-3 Sentences Each)

1. **How does it work?**
   Turn it on, choose your filter, and press the shutter button. There is no screen to check. When you want to see your photos, plug Retra into your phone or laptop with the included USB-C cable.

2. **I have an iPhone with a Lightning port. How do I transfer photos?**
   iPhones with a Lightning port cannot transfer photos over a direct USB-C to Lightning cable. To view your photos, use an SD card reader with a Lightning adapter, or plug Retra into a computer with the included USB cable and transfer them to your phone.

3. **What is the image quality like?**
   Retra is designed with a vintage, film-style aesthetic rather than modern smartphone perfection. Photos feature natural grain, warm colors, and retro digital character reminiscent of classic point-and-shoots and disposable cameras.

4. **Do I need to develop the photos?**
   No. Retra is completely digital, so there are no film cartridges to buy, no darkrooms, and no development fees. All photos save as standard JPEG files to the included memory card.

5. **How many photos does it hold, and how long does the battery last?**
   The included memory card holds about 500 photos, and the built-in rechargeable battery lasts for roughly 500 shots per charge. Once you transfer your photos to your device, clear the card and you are ready to shoot again.

6. **What is in the box?**
   Each box contains your Retra camera, an SD memory card, a USB charging and transfer cable, a wrist strap, a quick start guide, and a complimentary gift ribbon.
