### The 4 Filter Profiles & Creative Direction

#### 1. Natural Profile
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Crisp, true-to-life tones with gentle highlight roll-off and natural contrast. Captures morning sea air and European cobblestones exactly as your naked eye saw them, completely avoiding the aggressive, unnatural sharpening of modern smartphone software.
- **Best Suited For:** Sunlit city architecture, scenic coastal walks and mountain trails, candid family portraits in open daylight.
- **Curator Note:** "Clean, balanced, and honest. The profile you leave on for everyday street walks."

#### 2. Vintage Profile
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Warm honeyed highlights, slightly faded shadows, and subtle cyan undertones reminiscent of 1970s magazine travel spreads and classic Kodachrome slides. Instantly adds decade-spanning nostalgia to sunny afternoons.
- **Best Suited For:** European terracotta rooftops, beach picnics, late afternoon golden hours, and outdoor dinners.
- **Curator Note:** "Turns an ordinary Tuesday afternoon into a memory from thirty summers ago."

#### 3. Black & White Profile
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Rich monochrome with velvety shadow depth, radiant silver highlights, and authentic analog grain structure. Strips away color distraction to highlight pure light, architecture, texture, and facial expressions.
- **Best Suited For:** Evening city walks, train stations, café conversations, and intimate celebratory dinners.
- **Curator Note:** "Timeless, dramatic, and unflinchingly candid. Pure light and shadow."

#### 4. Film Profile
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Punchy, high-energy disposable-camera aesthetic with saturated reds and cobalt blues, high micro-contrast, and deliberate filmic imperfection. Delivers that iconic 1990s vacation photograph vibe straight out of the camera.
- **Best Suited For:** Theme-park rides, festival crowds, beach parties, flash-lit night adventures, and celebratory toasts.
- **Curator Note:** "Bright, bold, and electric. Perfect for when you want flash and fun."

### The 4-Step Process

1. **Step 01: Power On & Check Your Counter**
   - Flip the mechanical power toggle on the top plate.
   - The minimalist OLED status window illuminates quietly, displaying your remaining battery life and the number of shots available on your included memory card.
   - No setup menus, no account logins, and no pairing Bluetooth apps required.

2. **Step 02: Select Your Analog Color Profile**
   - Click the dedicated filter selector to choose how your frame will be rendered: Natural, Vintage, Black & White, or Film.
   - The selected color science is mapped straight to the optical JPEG file as you shoot.
   - You can switch profiles between any two frames in half a second.

3. **Step 03: Frame, Click, and Return to Life**
   - Lift the camera to your eye and look through the clear optical glass viewfinder.
   - Press the tactile mechanical shutter button.
   - Feel the physical snap.
   - Put Retra back into your pocket or let it rest against your wrist strap.
   - There is no screen to check, no rating to give, and no urge to retake it sixteen times. You took the memory; now you keep living it.

4. **Step 04: Plug In and Relive the Surprise**
   - At the end of your trip, weekend, or holiday, connect Retra directly to your computer, iPad, or smartphone using the included high-speed USB-C cable.
   - Alternatively, slide out the included SD memory card and insert it into a pocket card reader.
   - Open your photo folders and experience the genuine magic of delayed gratification: unscripted smiles, candid gestures, and the real spirit of your days waiting to be discovered.
