## Accordion 4: Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How does the Retra camera work?
Retra is engineered to be delightfully simple. Turn the power switch on, choose one of the four built-in film color profiles, look through the optical viewfinder, and press the shutter button. An integrated OLED display on the top plate shows you how many frames you have taken. There is no screen on the back to review your shots. You simply shoot, put the camera back in your pocket, and stay fully in the moment. When you get back to your hotel or home, plug the camera directly into your laptop, tablet, or phone with the included USB-C cable to see your photos.

### 2. I have an older iPhone with a Lightning port. How do I get my photos onto my phone?
Apple's older Lightning port does not deliver the power needed to read external storage drives over a simple USB-C to Lightning cable. If your iPhone uses a Lightning port, you have two quick ways to view your photos:
- Option A (Most popular for travel): Remove the included SD card from Retra and insert it into a standard Lightning SD card reader adapter. Plug the adapter into your iPhone, open the Photos app, and tap "Import."
- Option B: Plug Retra into any Mac, PC, or iPad using the included USB-C cable, save your photos, and send them to your iPhone via AirDrop or iCloud.
*(Note: If you have an iPhone 15 or newer with a USB-C port, you can plug Retra directly into your phone with the included cable.)*

### 3. What does the image quality look like?
Retra is intentionally engineered to evoke the warm, nostalgic aesthetic of classic disposable film cameras and early 2000s digital point-and-shoots. The 8MP sensor produces crisp, rich images with natural grain, gentle edge softness, and authentic color rendering. It does not look like a sterile, over-processed 48MP smartphone photo that has been artificially sharpened by an algorithm. Your photos look like genuine memories, complete with the honest warmth and character of real life.

### 4. Do I ever need to buy film or pay a lab for processing?
Never. Retra gives you the ritual, look, and delayed gratification of film without any of the recurring expenses or delays. There are no film rolls to buy, no darkrooms to locate, and no $20 lab processing invoices per roll. The camera captures digital JPEG files onto the included reusable memory card. You can shoot thousands of photos over the lifetime of the camera without spending an extra dollar on film or developing.

### 5. How many photos can I take before the battery dies or memory fills up?
On a single full battery charge, Retra powers roughly 500 photos, which is more than enough for a busy weekend getaway, a marathon day at a theme park, or several beach days. The included memory card also stores approximately 500 full-resolution images. Once you transfer your photos to your computer or hard drive, simply clear the memory card and Retra is ready for another 500 frames.

### 6. What comes inside the box?
Every Retra order arrives safely packed in an archival gift box and includes:
- 1x Retra Screen-Free Camera
- 1x Pre-installed high-speed MicroSD storage card (stores ~500 photos)
- 1x USB-C high-speed charging and data transfer cable
- 1x Woven cotton wrist lanyard
- 1x Quick-start field manual
- 1x Signature navy herringbone gift ribbon and keepsake card
