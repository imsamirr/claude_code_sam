## Part D: Visual Branding & Layout Guide (Retro-pia Aesthetic Implementation)

For the designer and developer laying out this landing page:

1. **Color Palette:**
   - **Background Base:** Warm Cream (#FAF8F5 / #F5F2EB)
   - **Deep Primary Ink:** Midnight Navy (#0E1B2A)
   - **Secondary Text / Muted Lines:** Soft Charcoal Slate (#5A6675)
   - **Accent Tone:** Vintage Terracotta (#C86D51) or Olive Ochre (#8C855C) for subtle badges and tags
2. **Typography System:**
   - **Display Headings:** Editorial Serif with high x-height and gentle personality (e.g., Ogg, IvyPresto, or Playfair Display).
   - **Two-Line Headline Treatment:** Line 1 is bold primary serif; Line 2 is italicized or slightly smaller slate serif/sans.
   - **Kicker Eyebrows:** Clean, tracked-out uppercase mono or sans-serif (e.g., `[ THE UNEDITED ARCHIVE ]`).
   - **Body Copy:** Legible, warm editorial sans-serif (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk, Inter, or GT America).
3. **Imagery Direction:**
   - Emphasize rich analog grain, soft lens flares, candid movement, and genuine human emotion.
   - Avoid sterile e-commerce white backdrops; photograph the camera on worn linen, wooden outdoor tables, hotel nightstands, and sandy beach towels.
