Frequently asked questions

How does Retra work?

Turn it on, choose your filter, look through the optical viewfinder, and press the shutter button. There is no back screen to review your shots. When you want to see your photos, plug Retra into your phone or laptop with the included USB-C cable.

I have an iPhone with a Lightning port. How do I transfer photos?

iPhones with a Lightning port cannot transfer photos over a direct USB-C to Lightning cable. You can insert the included SD card into a Lightning card reader adapter to import directly, or plug Retra into a computer and sync the files.

What is the image quality like?

Retra is built with a nostalgic, film-like aesthetic rather than smartphone sharpness. Images feature warm color tones, gentle grain, and retro digital character reminiscent of classic point-and-shoot cameras.

Do I need to buy film or pay a lab for developing?

No. Retra is fully digital, so there are no film rolls to buy and zero development fees. All photos save as standard JPEG files to the included SD memory card.

How many photos can I take on one charge?

The built-in rechargeable lithium battery powers about 500 shots per charge, and the included memory card holds roughly 500 photos. Once you offload your photos to a computer or phone, simply clear the card and shoot again.

What comes inside the box?

Every box includes your Retra camera, an SD memory card, a USB charging and transfer cable, a wrist strap, a quick start guide, and a complimentary gift ribbon.

Does the camera have a flash for night photos?

Yes. Retra includes a built-in LED flash that gives evening gatherings, parties, and night walks that classic, high-contrast direct-flash film look.

Can I change between filters while shooting?

Yes. You can switch between Natural, Vintage, Black & White, and Film whenever you like. The selected profile applies straight to each JPEG photo as you take it.
