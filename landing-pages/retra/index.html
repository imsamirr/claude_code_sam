Description

Memories you can live inside.

The pocket camera built for the days you do not want to view through a phone.

Modern life turns every beautiful moment into a content assignment. You stand at a sunlit terrace in Florence, watch the fireworks break over a fairytale castle, or sit with your feet buried in warm coastal sand, and the instinct is always the same: pull out a glass screen.

Within seconds, the moment is lost. You take thirty nearly identical photos. You zoom in to inspect your smile. You see three unanswered work messages, a breaking news alert, and a friend's vacation story. Instead of living your afternoon, you are managing your phone.

Retra is our answer to screen fatigue.

It is a pocket-sized digital camera designed with the spirit, ritual, and patience of 35mm film. There is no LCD screen on the back. There is no camera roll to endlessly review. There are no menus to tap through, and zero notifications buzzing against your palm.

You hold the camera up, look through the optical viewfinder, and click the shutter button. That is it. The shot is captured, stored quietly in memory, and left for you to discover later.

Because you cannot check the photo, you stop fussing over perfection. You take the picture and return your eyes to the room. When you plug Retra into your laptop or phone at the end of the trip, you get to relive your holiday all over again: real laughter, candid expressions, golden hour light, and the honest, unposed warmth that smartphones smooth away.

Whether you are packing light for three weeks across Europe, navigating a bustling theme park with your family, or giving someone the gift of undivided presence, Retra keeps the focus exactly where it belongs: on the life in front of you.

FAQ
How does the Retra camera work?

Turn the power switch on, select one of the four built-in filters if desired, look through the optical viewfinder, and press the shutter button. A small OLED shot counter on the top plate displays your remaining frame count. There is no screen on the back to review your shots. You take the picture, put the camera back in your pocket, and stay fully in the moment. When you want to see your photos, connect Retra to your phone or laptop with the included USB-C cable.

I have an older iPhone with a Lightning port. How do I get my photos onto my phone?

iPhones with a Lightning port cannot transfer photos using a direct USB-C to Lightning cable. If your iPhone uses a Lightning port, you have two simple options:

  • Option A: Remove the included SD card from Retra and insert it into an SD card reader with a Lightning adapter, then plug it into your iPhone to import your photos into your camera roll.
  • Option B: Connect Retra to a laptop or computer using the included USB cable, save your photos, and transfer them to your phone.
What does the image quality look like?

Retra is intentionally engineered to evoke the warm, nostalgic aesthetic of classic disposable film cameras and early 2000s digital point-and-shoots. The 8MP sensor produces crisp, rich images with natural grain, gentle edge softness, and authentic color rendering. It does not look like a sterile, over-processed 48MP smartphone photo that has been artificially sharpened by an algorithm. Your photos look like genuine memories, complete with the honest warmth and character of real life.

Do I ever need to buy film or pay a lab for processing?

Never. Retra gives you the ritual, look, and delayed gratification of film without any of the recurring expenses or delays. There are no film rolls to buy, no darkrooms to locate, and no $20 lab processing invoices per roll. The camera captures digital JPEG files onto the included reusable memory card. You can shoot thousands of photos over the lifetime of the camera without spending an extra dollar on film or developing.

How many photos can I take before the battery dies or memory fills up?

On a single full battery charge, Retra powers roughly 500 photos, which is more than enough for a busy weekend getaway, a marathon day at a theme park, or several beach days. The included memory card also stores approximately 500 full-resolution images. Once you transfer your photos to your computer or hard drive, simply clear the memory card and Retra is ready for another 500 frames.

The ritual

How it works.Four tactile steps from your pocket to your memory vault.

  1. 01

    Power On & Check Your Counter

    Slide the power switch on. The small OLED shot counter turns on to display the remaining photo count on your included SD card. No account setup, passwords, or wireless pairing required.

  2. 02

    Select Your Analog Color Profile

    Click the dedicated filter selector to choose how your frame will be rendered: Natural, Vintage, Black & White, or Film.

  3. 03

    Frame, Click, and Return to Life

    Look through the optical viewfinder. Press the shutter button to take the photo. Put Retra back into your pocket or let it rest on your wrist strap. There is no preview screen to review. You captured the moment; now you keep living it.

  4. 04

    Plug In and Relive the Surprise

    When you are ready to see your photos, connect Retra to your phone or laptop using the included USB-C cable. Alternatively, take out the included SD memory card and insert it into a standard card reader. Open your photos and relive the memories you captured.
